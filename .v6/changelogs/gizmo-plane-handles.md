## Gizmo: rings of some axes, poses of frames

- `transformHandles`' `rotate` takes the axes whose rings are drawn (`[0, 1]`: a plane's tilts, no turn about its
  normal); `true` draws the three as before.
- `poseOfFrame` and `frameOfPose` (`@molstar/graphics/util/gizmo/gestures`): a rigid frame as the pose a drag starts
  from, and back.
