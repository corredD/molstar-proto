## Prism: an interval without bounds is two number fields

- A `PD.Interval` without `min` and `max` (the volume-value color theme's custom domain, formal and partial charge, and
  others) was a two-knob slider over 0..1: no value below 0 or above 1, and its default [-1, 1] not shown. It is now
  two number fields, from and to, that take any number (a comma as the decimal point), as v5's; a typed end past the
  other stops at it. An interval with both bounds keeps its slider.
