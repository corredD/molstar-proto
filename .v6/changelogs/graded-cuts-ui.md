## prism-me: graded cuts and Shown, in the app

- A clip's What it cuts gives each object or part of particles its way: Keeps a layer, Cuts it or Cuts it wider. A
  layer kept and a wider cut have a band (Å) and a fuzziness. A node's Clips shows the clip's way on it too. Each
  change is one step (`explorer.clipWay`).
- A graded way switches the scene's cuts to instance (Proteins) in the same step. The plain cut leaves the variant as
  it is.
- Look › Shown (%) on a group, an entity or a target shows a share of its particles, the same ones at every share. A
  target's Look has Shown only.
