## prism-me: Mesoscale

- Grow copies from an instance: a particle's right-click › Grow from this instance… (or the last copy painted) is the
  source; its list's Grow section grows copies by a rule, one step: Random walk (a new fiber), Crystal (a lattice in
  the source's frame), Tiling (Hex or Square, in its plane), Sphere (copies at random in a ball). The copies are the
  list's painted copies, saved in sessions; overlaps allowed. `mesoscale.grow { particles, index, mode, … }`.
