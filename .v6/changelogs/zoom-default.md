## prism-me: Mesoscale

- Colors by zoom on at load: the Mesoscale Explorer on Prism opens a cellPACK or PetWorld file (`mmcif_particles`: an
  example, a URL, a dropped file) with its colors following the zoom (compartment, entity, chain, atom; Look › By zoom
  On), its targets made so in the load's one build; Off still turns them off, one step. Prism's own viewer and
  Simularium files keep them off.
- By zoom's edges start at 0.27, 2.2 and 28.8 px (entity, chain, atom). By zoom stays on for a later theme. A Look
  color, a uniform color or Generate colors is that color until the atoms, then the element colors.
