## MesoViewSpec (prototype)

- `@molstar/meso-spec` and `@molstar/meso-molstar` (private, `packages/mesoscale`): a prototype of a declarative format
  for mesoscale scenes (biology, evidence, provenance, frames, view state), its IR, a runtime with pluggable resolvers
  (packing, lattices, fibers, bilayers, precomputed STAR placements, cellPACKgpu recipes) and a Prism adapter; stories
  under MesoViewSpec in Storybook. Design and renderer findings in `packages/mesoscale/_spec/`.
