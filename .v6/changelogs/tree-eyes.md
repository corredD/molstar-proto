## prism-me: the explorer's tree

- Every row shows its eye and its ⋯, always, each in a column of its own; the brush, the scissors and the trash are in
  the ⋯ (Color…, Add clip…, Remove) with the right-click's items, on a category too; no Hide in the menus. A row's color
  dot opens its colors.
- A parent's eye sets every row under it, all the way down, in one step (a model's entities, a list's targets, a
  category's objects); each row keeps its own state after (hide the model, then show one group alone). A parent's eye
  is open when all under it is shown.
- Press an eye and drag along the column: every row passed takes the first row's new state, one undo step.
- Show only this, its group, its compartment, and Show all again, on a part's ⋯ and right-click: one step each.
- `cryoet.decorations` says each target's `visible` (a layer of it alone hiding it): a list's class has its eye.
