# EMPIAR by id: browse an entry live and load what it has (2026-11-04, lane `empiar`)

Planned, not started (Ludo, 2026-11-04: "could we access EMPIAR like EMPIAR-12534 the same way we access cryoET data
portal?", then "could we just have the worker fill in the ui once provided with a empiar id, and then pick what we want
to load from whats available?"). Started on Ludo's word: the lane copies this brief to its `progress/<date>-empiar.md`
and builds from there.

## Brief

- **Asked:** an EMPIAR id in, the entry's contents out as a picker, what is picked loaded. No index built beforehand:
  the browser reads the entry live.
- **Check** (on the static build, no server of ours): open `?empiar=12534` (or type `EMPIAR-12534` in + Open). The card
  shows the title, the two conditions (CHX, vehicle) and, unfolded, each tomogram run with its files and their sizes.
  Pick run `YTC041_1_lam1_1_ts_003`: its tomogram's slices, its membrane labels, and its 80S ribosomes load in one step,
  the ribosomes in the tomogram's densities, EMD-48752 (80S) as their decoration. The `.hff` surfaces show, greyed out,
  why they can't load.
- **Decision** (2026-11-04): no index, unlike the portal. EMPIAR's REST API and its file listings both send
  `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, so a browser can walk an entry. The portal's GraphQL can't be used from a browser,
  so the portal needed an index; EMPIAR doesn't.
- **Spec:** new `extensions/cryoet/_spec/empiar.md`: the walk, the grouping into runs, the kinds, the load, the frames
  and units, the size guards. `apps/prism-me/_spec/features.md` gains the EMPIAR rows; `_spec/ui.md` the card, in + Open
  (the explorer's four places).
- **Touches:** `packages/extensions/cryoet` (an `empiar` source and its operations beside `portal`; its card),
  `apps/prism-me/src/url.ts` (`?empiar=`). Likely a library lane: ranged MRC slices (step C), only if step C goes that
  way.
- **Base and delivery:** prism-me only, from `origin/prism-me`; a library part (step C) is its own lane from
  `origin/master`.

## What was found (2026-11-04, by curl)

**The example: EMPIAR-12534**, "Cytoplasmic ribosomes on mitochondria alter the local membrane environment for protein
import", 764.2 GB, released 2025-03-17, EMD-48751 and EMD-48752 (both subtomogram averages of the 80S, 3.33 Å).

| Piece               | Where (under `data/EMPIAR_upload/`, and `…_nonCHX/` for the vehicle-treated)                | Format, size                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tomograms           | `Reconstructed_tomograms/<grid>/<run>.mrc_9.98Apx.mrc`; 38 CHX, 49 vehicle (15.83 Å)        | MRC mode 2, 682×960×334 at 9.98 Å, origin 0: 834 MB; a z-slice 2.62 MB       |
| Tilt series         | `Tilt_series/<grid>/<run>.mrc.st`                                                           | MRC, 5760×4092, 1.663 Å, unaligned                                           |
| Tilt angles         | `Tilt_series_rawtlt/<grid>/<run>.mrc.rawtlt`; `PACE_mdocs/`                                 | text, one angle a line (−57 to 54 by 3)                                      |
| Particles           | `Particle_lists/80S_ribosome_CHX.star` (12 MB), `co-translation_ribosome_CHX.star` (493 KB) | RELION 3 (Warp): `rlnCoordinateX/Y/Z` in pixels of `rlnPixelSize` **4.99 Å** |
| Voxel segmentations | `Voxel_segmentations/<grid>/<run>_labels.mrc`                                               | MRC labels                                                                   |
| Surfaces            | `Surfaces/<grid>/<run>_labels.hff`                                                          | EMDB-SFF (HDF5)                                                              |
| Frames              | `Frames/` (462 and 492 tilts, 11 or 12 frames each)                                         | TIFF; never loaded                                                           |

**EMPIAR:**

- **API** `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/empiar/api/entry/EMPIAR-<id>/`: title, authors, cross-references (EMDB), and the image
  sets: each its directory, category (`reconstructed volumes`, `tilt series`, `micrographs - multiframe`), format, image
  size and pixel size. `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`.
- **Files** `https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/empiar/world_availability/<id>/data/…`: a folder is an HTML listing (name, date, size
  as `834M`, `12M`, `493K`); `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, `Range` allowed and answered `206` (so an MRC's header is
  its first 1 KB, and a z-slice a range).
- **The layout is the depositor's.** Image sets name the folders they describe; the rest (`Particle_lists`, `Surfaces`,
  `Voxel_segmentations`) only by name and extension. A run is a file stem shared across folders
  (`YTC041_1_lam1_1_ts_003`). A particle names its tomogram by `rlnMicrographName` (`<run>.mrc.tomostar`).
- **The particles are at another scale than the tomogram:** 4.99 Å pixels (bin 2) against the tomogram's 9.98 Å. The
  scale comes from the STAR's `rlnPixelSize`, never from the tomogram.

**prism-me today:** reads MRC (`reader/ccp4`, whole files), RELION STAR particles (`reader/relion`,
`formats/particles/star.ts`), the EMDB averages (`load.entry`, source `emdb`). No HDF5 (`.hff`). No reader of MRC by
ranges.

## Steps

**A. The walk and the picker (no new reader).**

1. `empiar.entry(id)`: the API's entry, its image sets and cross-references; then the folders read on demand (a folder
   when its row unfolds), each listing parsed (name, size, folder or file) by a small parser of the listing's anchors
   and sizes. Answers that come late go through `latest`, keyed by folder (rendering.md §2.7): an old listing never
   lands over a newer one. A read changes nothing in the scene. Test: the recorded API reply and listings of 12534
   (fixtures, never the network) give 2 conditions, 5 grids each, the runs of `YTC041_1`.
2. Grouping: a run is a stem; each file's kind by its image set (its directory), else by its folder's name and its
   extension (`.mrc` under `Voxel_segmentations` a mask, `.star` particles, `.rawtlt` angles, `.hff` surfaces, `.tif`
   frames). A file nothing recognises is listed under its folder, loadable as what its extension says, or not at all,
   saying why. Test: every file of the fixture lands in one run or one folder, none twice.
3. The card in + Open (`_spec/ui.md`, the explorer's four places): the id field, then the entry's tree, each file with
   its size, loadable or greyed with the reason. Storybook story, light and dark, wide and narrow.
4. `?empiar=<id>`, `?empiar-run=<stem>`, read the way `url.ts` reads the portal's.

Gate: on the static build, `?empiar=12534` lists the entry and its runs; nothing loads until picked.

**B. The load, one step.**

1. `empiar.load({ id, run, files })`: one operation, one history step, the size asked first above the portal's threshold
   (`PortalLargeBytes`: "834 MB, load anyway?"). Tomogram and masks by `load.url` as MRC; the tilt series as the
   portal's (its Z slice stepped, its angles from the `.rawtlt`); the STAR's particles of that run
   (`rlnMicrographName`'s stem) by the particles source, `pixelSize` from the STAR; the EMDB cross-references as their
   decoration (`load.entry`).
2. The particles' frame: a known particle of the fixture lands at a known point of the tomogram's frame (in Å), and its
   Euler angles (ZYZ, RELION's) give a known orientation. Then in the app: the 80S densities of `YTC041_1_lam1_1_ts_003`
   hold the ribosomes; and EMD-48752's hand against the tomogram's (the portal's 10003 had a mirror: portal.md §3).
3. Test: the fixture's run loads its tomogram, its mask and its particles in one step, undone in one.

Gate: Ludo's check above, on the static build.

**C. A tomogram too big to load whole (decide after B).**

An 834 MB tomogram exceeds what the explorer should load by default. Two ways, Ludo's call after B:

- **Slices by range** (a library lane): an MRC reader that reads the 1 KB header, then the z-slices asked for by `Range`
  (2.62 MB each), so the Z slice can step through the tomogram without the whole file; a coarse 3D view from every 4th
  slice (about 220 MB here). Upstream candidate.
- **Converted once** (a tool, as `tools/portal-index`): a script that writes small OME-Zarr copies of chosen entries,
  served by us. Simple in the browser, but a server's files to keep.
- **Binned on the fly** (Ludo, 2026-11-04, proposed; not decided: "start by full resolution, and we will optimize
  after"): above the size threshold, the prompt offers bin 4 (default), bin 2 or full; the MRC is streamed (header, then
  a slab of 2 or 4 z-slices at a time averaged into the smaller grid), never held whole. 12534's tomogram: bin 2
  341×480×167 at 19.96 Å, about 105 MB; bin 4 170×240×83 at 39.9 Å, about 13 MB. Memory only: the 834 MB still downloads
  (reading every 2nd or 4th slice by `Range` cuts it, z subsampled not averaged). Then, with slices by range: the binned
  volume for the overview and 3D, a slice at rest fetched at full resolution (2.6 MB). A streaming reader in
  `packages/io/src/reader/ccp4` (upstream candidate), lane `mrc-bin`. The particles are unaffected (their Å come from
  the STAR's `rlnPixelSize`).

**D. Later, on Ludo's word.**

- `.hff` surfaces: an HDF5 reader (h5wasm, lazy-loaded, its size against the bundle budget) and EMDB-SFF's meshes.
- Other entries' layouts: check 2 or 3 more cryo-ET entries before calling the grouping general.
- The assistant: `empiar.entry` and `empiar.load` as its tools ("load the ribosomes of YTC041_1 from EMPIAR-12534"), as
  every operation is.

## Risks

- **Every entry's layout differs:** the grouping fails soft (anything unknown is still listed), and the fixture covers
  one entry only; step D checks others.
- **The listing is HTML, not an API:** its format can change; the parser is small, tested on the recorded listings, and
  a failure says the folder could not be read rather than showing nothing.
- **Size:** the tomograms are big, with no smaller copy; the size is asked first, and step C decides the rest.
- **The particles' convention:** RELION 3 from Warp, at bin 2; step B.2 tests the frame before anything is built on it.
