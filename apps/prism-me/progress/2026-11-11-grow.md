# Lane `grow`: growing copies from an instance (2026-11-11)

Plan: `apps/prism-me/plans/2026-11-10-paint-on-density.md`, revised order (Ludo, 2026-11-11): "lets start by the
growing. If I paint an instance, or I click an instance, I should have a growing options (randomwalk for fiber, crystal
growth, symetry growth, tiling growth, random growth in a sphere). Then placement and growing can be constraint on mesh
surface first then weighted by density if one selected."

Branch `me/grow` from `origin/me/paint` (04e7f65cf; PR #64, the paint layer merged into it). Local until Ludo signs off.

## Brief

**What Ludo sees.**

1. Right-click a particle: **Grow from this instance…** beside Move this instance and Offset all like it. While
   painting, the last copy painted is the source as well.
2. The **Grow** block (in Paint, the list's section) names its source, "From CA #1204", and offers:
   - **Mode**: Random walk, Crystal, Symmetry, Tiling, Sphere.
   - Each mode's own params (below), a **Seed**, and **Grow**.
3. Grow adds the copies to the source's list, as painted copies: the source's target and entity, its list's particles,
   saved in sessions. One undo step a grow.
4. Copies go **exactly where the rule says; overlaps are allowed** (Ludo, 2026-11-11). A relax comes later
   (bucket-and-relax plan, B).

**Modes.**

| Mode        | Params (defaults)                                                                                  | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Random walk | Steps (20), Persistence (30° a step)                                                               | A fiber from the source: a persistent random walk, one rise a step (the entity's fiber settings: its rise, else touching), its direction turned at most Persistence a step, seeded. Stored as a fiber stroke, so its units are made by `makeFiber`, its rise and twist kept. |
| Crystal     | a, b, c (Å; default the copy's diameter), α, β, γ (90°), na, nb, nc (3, 3, 1)                      | A lattice in the source's own frame: X + R·(i·a + j·b + k·c) for i < na, j < nb, k < nc (centered on the source), each copy turned as the source.                                                                                                                            |
| Symmetry    | round 1: none. Round 2: Point (Cn, Dn, T, O, I; radius), Helical (rise, twist, count), Space group | Round 2: copies by the operators about an axis through the source, and the structure's own space group and cell (its symmetry mates; a structure brush or a structure target only).                                                                                          |
| Tiling      | Pattern (Hex, Square), Spacing (the copy's diameter), Rings (3)                                    | A planar tiling in the source's own tangent plane (normal: its axis, the membrane's or its principal one), ring by ring, each copy turned as the source. On a mesh in lane `grow-mesh`.                                                                                      |
| Sphere      | Count (50), Radius (5 × the copy's radius), Turn (Random, As the source)                           | Count copies uniform in a ball about the source (seeded); turned at random or as the source.                                                                                                                                                                                 |

**Rounds.**

- **Round 1**: the engine (`grow.ts`: a mode → the copies' poses in the source's list frame), `mesoscale.grow`, the Grow
  block and the right-click, and four modes: Random walk, Crystal, Tiling (plane), Sphere.
- **Round 2**: Symmetry: point groups, helical, and the structure's space group (`ModelSymmetry`, the spacegroup helpers
  in core/math/geometry/spacegroup).

**After this lane (the plan, revised).**

- `grow-mesh`: placement and growth constrained to a mesh surface (OBJ, PLY, VTP first: `getMeshTriangleLookup`, the
  triangle's normal). Paint on a mesh, Tiling and Random walk on it, Sphere's points projected to it.
- `density-weight`: the fit (D1 of the plan: a mini envelope match in a reach), then growth weighted by density when a
  volume is chosen: each grown copy fitted, kept above a threshold.

## Where it plugs in

- The source:
  - `particleBehind` / `area.particleOf` (apps/prism-me/src/explorer/ui.tsx:160) gives `{ list, index }`.
  - Its pose comes from the list as drawn (its decorators'), brought back to the list's frame (the paint operation's
    `toListFrame`, mesoscale/src/operations.ts ~772). **Check the frame**: a moved list's drawn coordinates are after
    its Transform; strokes are stored before it.
  - Its target and entity come from `brushOf`-like reads of that particle.
- The copies: one `PaintStroke` (mesoscale/src/paint.ts:38), explicit positions and rotations (or a fiber stroke for
  Random walk), plus a `grow: { mode, params, seed, source }` record for the report. It is committed with
  `updatePainted` (one step), as `mesoscale.paint` does.
- A structure source: its copies stay its entity's (`PaintStructure`), as painted copies of a structure do.

## Tests (each first failing)

- Crystal: na·nb·nc copies (the source counted once), each at X + R·t, turned as the source; a moved list's copies land
  in the list's frame.
- Tiling: Hex with r rings gives 1 + 3r(r+1) points, all in the source's plane, a spacing apart; Square gives (2r+1)².
- Sphere: Count copies, all within Radius; the same seed, the same copies.
- Random walk: a fiber stroke of Steps points a rise apart, each turn ≤ Persistence; seeded.
- The operation: one undo step; the source's target and entity; a session keeps the copies; refused for a particle that
  is not there.
- The right-click shows Grow from this instance on a particle.

## Built (round 1)

- `grow.ts`: the engine, pure: `crystalPoses` (a cell of a, b, c, α, β, γ; na·nb·nc − 1 copies), `tilingPoses` (Hex,
  Square, ring by ring), `spherePoses` (uniform in a ball, seeded; Random or As the source), `walkPath` (a persistent
  random walk, uniform in the Persistence cone), and the frames: `poseToScene`, `rotationsToListFrame`, `offsetPose`.
- `mesoscale.grow { particles, index, mode, … }`: one stroke of the source list's `ParticleListPainted` (one step), with
  a `grow` record (mode, params, seed, source index and key); a walk is a fiber stroke (made by `makeFiber`). A command
  (⌘K) and an assistant tool.
- `MesoscaleApi.growSource` / `setGrowSource` / `growSourceState` / `growSettings`; the paint host's stroke makes its
  last copy the source.
- The Grow section (`GrowBlock`): From, Mode, the mode's params (lengths unset: the copy's size), Seed, Grow.
- A particle's right-click: Grow from this instance… (opens the Grow section).

## Decided

- (Ludo, 2026-11-11) Copies go exactly where the rule says, overlaps allowed; one step a grow; the copies are the list's
  painted copies.
- (author) The source is read where the painted copies are (the Painted decorator's output, list frame), lifted through
  the list's Transform (scale, then the matrix) and its own Move this instance (review S1); Crystal, Tiling and Sphere
  grow in the scene's Å and come back by `toListFrame` and the conjugate turn. The offsets of its kind are not read: the
  copies take them too, so a translation offset keeps the pattern on the source as drawn.
- (author) The Grow block is a section of its own on a list's rows (list, model, target, group, entity), not inside
  Paint: Paint shows only on a single-entity row, and a STAR list's rows are targets. **A change from the brief.**
- (author) Counts: Crystal's offsets `i − floor((n−1)/2)` (the source a lattice point, an even n one more after);
  Tiling's totals count the source; a walk's first point is a rise from the source along its fiber axis (the Fiber Axis
  when set, review S6); a grow of no copy or a walk of one point is refused (review S2, N1); at most 20000 copies a
  grow.
- (author) Copy diameter: its radius, else its structure's bounding sphere, else 10 Å. Another source unsets the lengths
  (review G1).

## Learned

- A section's open state is read once when it is made (`SectionBlock`): to open Grow from the menu the properties are
  made again (picked unset, then revealed).
- Operations run from a view are the person's: a refusal is posted as a warning already (skeptic, S5).
- A bank's fiber is all units (targets of the decorator's): no grow source, and a paint of one keeps the source before.
