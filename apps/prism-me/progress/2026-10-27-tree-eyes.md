# The tree's eyes under a hidden model (2026-10-27)

Lane `me/tree-eyes` (`~/prism-me-lanes/tree-eyes`), from Ludovic: "fix the eye visibility in the tree, especially
cellPACK and YASARA (PetWorld): if the parent eye is closed all children are closed, and we can reopen the eye of one
child and it will be visible".

## Built

- `rows.ts`: a model's group and entity rows show their eye closed while the model is hidden (`o.visible &&` their own),
  marked `hiddenAbove`. Opening one so: one `api.group` step: every entity hidden (`mesoscale.style`, entities empty),
  this part shown, the model shown (`particles.visibility`), in that order so the whole model never flashes.
- Test: `explorer/_test/tree-eyes.test.ts` (failed first: the parts' eyes stayed open under the hidden model).

## Decided

- Hiding the model keeps its parts' own flags (one cell hidden, not every layer written): shown again, it looks as it
  did. Opening a part under it is "this alone", as the request says.
- Only a model's parts: a list's targets have no eye in the tree, a structure's entities none either; a volume's
  representations are hidden with it by Mol*'s subtree visibility already.

## Size

Production ~25 lines, test ~150.
