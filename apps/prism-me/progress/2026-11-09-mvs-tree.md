## Decided

- The eye and Show only on document rows are built here (`mesoviewspec.eye`, `.showOnly`), not as `.look` (§5.3's style
  looks are lane `mvs-drive`'s): the coordinator's check asked for them, and §11 question 3 says saved (ours).
- Part rows have no eye (§11 question 12: read-only in 3D); a superstructure's envelope is the outer row itself (ours).
- Two populations of one entity are labelled with their id (`catalase (catalse) · p.catalase`) (ours, seen in the app).
- A model edit rebuilds the whole outline, not only the rows whose counts changed (§4.1): the tree keeps the DOM of
  every row that is the same (`keepRows`) (ours).
- The hover label is the lists' own (`getParticleLabel`, the type's description), no second label provider (sequence.md
  §10's one label) (ours).
- A document copy is no distance point in selection mode (no object row to hang it under): open, TODO.md (ours).
- Focus frames `fit` only; the view's selection highlight and Keep as the view's selection: lane `mvs-views` (TODO.md).

## Learned

- The document's lists left the objects, so the tree followed nothing when a document came alone: it follows the
  document's lists and the history (a document drawing no list included).
- `flattenInstances` gave an expansion only its template's looks; carrying an instance's hide by appending broke "later
  looks win" and hid copies of a template met twice: the decision needs the view's order (`visibleAt`).
- The adapter evicted the expansions a two-level one repeats, so its copies had no name or loci.
- The runtime's issue says `p.catalase: 354 of 506 placed`: matching ids on boundaries must let a closing `:` or `.` be.
- The app's `rows.scaling` eye-keys guard reads ×9 on this machine about one run in three (untouched code); the tree's
  band is 10 (Map growth past the cache), with its controls at ×14 and more.
