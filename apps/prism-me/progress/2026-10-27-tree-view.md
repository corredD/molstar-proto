## Built

The explorer's tree is the mock's view (`src/explorer/tree-view.tsx`, `tree.module.css`): Prism UI only, no viewer, so
the Storybook mock draws its fake scene with the same code (`_stories/explorer-panel.tsx` keeps its fake rows, menus and
properties). `ui.tsx` keeps the scene's reads, the picks and the menus; `rows.ts` the rows, now with their open state, a
category's objects and the clips.

- Rows on `VirtualList`, kept by `keepRows` with a field-by-field `sameRow` (no JSON per row, no `For` over every row):
  an opened model makes a screenful (2,400 entities in jsdom: under 120 rows made; mg.bcif in Chrome: 464 rows, 46
  made).
- Keys by Prism's `TreeNavigation` (↑/↓, Home, End, → opens or goes in, ← closes or goes to the parent, Enter); the pick
  follows the focus; a category is picked like any row. The eye's deferred run and the 3D pick's scroll are scoped
  `frames()` and `restartable()`; `treeKey`, `stableRows` and the raw `requestAnimationFrame`/`setTimeout` are gone.
- A row: an object's icon, color dot, name (the fade), count faint, then brush, scissors, eye, ⋯ on hover or focus,
  always on `@media (hover: none)`, a hidden node's eye kept; ⋯ opens the row's right-click menu under it. A label's row
  says `label` faint; a distance's reads `Distance 68.99 Å`.
- A category's eye (`state.visibility` of its objects, one step) and scissors (one Prism cut of all of them).
- Clips are rows (`Plane 1`) under the row they were made at (object, entity, chain, group, target; a category for a cut
  of several objects); their eye draws the cut in the scene (`clipping.show`).
- The filter keeps a hit with the rows it is in and drops a category with no hit (`filterRows`, shared with the mock).
- - Open: Files…, PDB / PDB-IHM / EMDB id…, Examples…, Session…, no icons; the empty line one text with the mock's.
- The split: `drag()` + `frameState`, clamped to 80 px each side, ↑/↓ by 16 px (Shift 64).
- `apps/prism-me` has `test:scaling` (`vitest.scaling.config.ts`) and its first guard, `rows.scaling.ts`.

## Learned

- jsdom lays nothing out: the panel's `height: 100%` was auto in the app's tab and the tree had no height, which every
  unit test passed (`dbcdbd2d5`). Check a virtual list in Chrome.
- The app's UI is in a shadow root: Playwright's locators reach it, `document.querySelector` does not.
- A scaling guard on a path with a large fixed cost needs a large n: an `indexOf` per row read ×6.7 at 4,000 (under the
  ×8 band), ×9.7 at 12,000.
- `state.visibility` takes several refs (`#a, #b`) as one step; `clipping.add`'s `node` too, as one cut.
