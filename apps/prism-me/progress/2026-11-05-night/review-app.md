## Summary

- **Real bugs, with probes.**
  - One **Remove** of an object that has labels makes N+1 undo steps. The first undo brings back a label with no object.
  - Prism's own remove (the one ⌘K and the agent use) leaves the label visible, with no tree row from which to remove
    it.
  - The properties panel still shows a removed object and acts on it.
  - The list's **Clip whole** choice is lost:
    - when another clip is added to the list (Proteins becomes pixel);
    - when a graded way is set on any clip (Atoms reads Proteins, but `clipPrimitive` stays on, hidden).
  - Hovering or picking a large group row is quadratic in its entities, measured at 10 ms, 144 ms and 2.2 s for 1k, 4k
    and 16k entities.
- **Interactions between lanes.**
  - The labels lane and the particle-handles lane meet in the canvas right-click: on a label in front of a particle, the
    label's **Move** is replaced by the particle's handle items (found by reading the code).
  - Graded cuts and the stage's Clip whole write the same `clip.variant`, with no owner, the same pattern as the looks
    cell had (architecture §5.1).
- **Duplication.**
  - Prism's cut-shape names are copied three times in the app.
  - Prism's `volume.visibility`/`particles.visibility {node}` are copied as `explorer.representationVisibility`.
  - `canvas.handles` is bypassed by `setHandleLook`.
  - Prism's `CutForm` is redone as `ClipSliders`.
  - `stagePicked`'s particle match is copied as `particleOf`.
  - Smaller copies: `rootOf` ×3, `Percent` with the slider line ×2, the object-kind sets ×3.
- **Dead code: little.**
  - `ExplorerApi.clicks` is used only by `measure.test.ts`.
  - About ten exports are used only in their own file (`keysUnder`, `compartmentOf`, `handlesOn`, `measureGesture`,
    `ParticleTypeLabels`, `MesoscaleHandleLook`, `changedValues`, `Section`, `SceneModel`, `kindName`). They are nits
    and get no row.
  - Every class in `tree.module.css`, `properties.module.css` and `explorer.module.css` is used.
- **#55 (slice picking): the app does not rely on `mapping.index` being a Map.**
  - Nothing under `apps/prism-me/src` reads `image.meta.mapping`.
  - The app meets slice picks in two places only:
    - `stagePicked` (stage-tools `operations.ts:912-921`) turns any `Volume.Cell`/`Volume.Loci` pick into the volume's
      row, which is a whole-volume `Volume.Loci` (`explorer/operations.ts:969-974`). Its highlight goes through
      `applySliceObjectLoci` and never through the index.
    - A distance's voxel point is a `Volume.Cell.Loci` (stage-tools `distance.ts:131`), marked through `eachSlice` →
      `mapping.index.get`, now `gridSliceIndex`.
  - So the app depends only on the computed index being right. `slice.test.ts` checks the index against the old Map over
    these cases:
    - x-fastest and z-fastest axis orders;
    - a space without strides (the lazy-Map path);
    - periodic volumes;
    - every slice axis and every theme.
  - `getCoords` inverts `dataOffset` for any axis order, so permuted MRC spaces are covered by construction.
  - No finding.
- **Already recorded, so not repeated here:**
  - root walks and plane types (architecture §5.4);
  - the JSON rebuild of the scene list;
  - each clip editor finding its cut by tag;
  - target ids sent as entities;
  - `gestureSteps`' 600 ms quiet and its history label;
  - the tree being a copy of Prism's `SceneTree`;
  - no CI for the mocks.

## Interactions traced (one scenario each)

- **Tree against properties:** a removed object stays in the properties. See A3.
- **Labels against particle handles:** see A4.
- **Graded cuts against Clip whole:** see A5 and A6.
- **`gestureSteps` forms against undo.**
  - Scenario: drag a Representations slider (an isosurface level), release, and press ⌘Z within 600 ms.
  - The step's `api.group` is still open, so Undo waits for the quiet and then undoes the whole gesture.
  - The form's `effect(representationParams)` does not load over a step in progress (`live.overlay('')`), and it loads
    the undone values once the step ends.
  - Verdict: correct. The 600 ms quiet and the drag-held-still case are already in TODO.md, "Live sliders". No row.
- **Labels against selections.**
  - In selection mode, a ⌘/Ctrl+click on a label goes to the picker with `EmptyLoci` (`ui.tsx:129`) and is refused as a
    distance point, with a message (`measure.ts:162`), as ui.md §7 says.
  - Add a label at residue granularity takes `finerThanRow` (`menu.ts:393`). It labels the residue under its row's key,
    so the label row sits under the chain or entity row it was made at.
  - Verdict: correct. No row.
- **Zoom colours against Look colours.**
  - With By zoom on (the default at load, `withZoomColorsAtLoad`), a Look colour picked on a model or group is an
    overpaint. It holds until the atoms, and `mesoscale.colorLevels`' run message says so (mesoscale
    `operations.ts:517-519`).
  - A later category theme leaves By zoom on.
  - Verdict: as the spec says (ui.md §4). No row.
- **UI scale:** the tree's rows, icons and indent follow `--msp-ui-scale` (`tree.module.css`). The label editor does
  not: see E3. The split line's 80 px and 240 px are also unscaled (a nit, no row).
