## Summary

- **Moves and clips are the weak joint (F1–F4).** "An object moved, its clips with it" works only through `stage.move`:
  - A structure dragged by its handle goes straight to Prism's `structure.transform`. Its clip and its label stay
    behind. Typed numbers (`stage.pose`) carry them. **Probed.**
  - A mesh's clip never moves. `movedRepresentationParams` reads only `type.params.clip`, and a shape keeps its clip at
    `clip`. **Probed.**
  - A clip shared by two objects splits when one of them moves. It is still listed as one cut (by its tag), but it sits
    in two places. **Probed.**
  - `stage.clipWhole` and Prism's scene-wide clip variant both write `clip.variant`, so they overwrite each other.
    `clipping.add` resets "Objects" to pixel, and a graded way on any list resets "Elements" elsewhere. This is the same
    pattern as the three writers of one looks cell (§7.1). **Probed.**
- **Colours by zoom:** Look › By zoom on a list whose theme is uniform lights the button and writes `colorLevels: on`,
  but a uniform theme draws no levels (color-levels.md: "Off for a uniform theme"). **Probed.** The other order (By zoom
  first, then the brush's Uniform) is handled and tested.
- **Performance:** `mesoEntities` is cached by list object. Every `stage.move`, `stage.scale` and `cryoet.offset` makes
  a new list object, so the tree's watched `infoState` recounts every particle: about 90 ms at 2M particles, after each
  release. The arrays it reads (`entities`, `targets`) are kept by the decorators, so a cache keyed on them would hit.
  **Measured.**
- **Dead code:** most of `mesoscale/src/prism-viewer-ui.tsx`, the stage's own right-click menu and part of `drag.ts` are
  unreachable in prism-me, the only app that loads these extensions. `distanceOf` and `distanceProps` are used nowhere.
- **Duplication** (Ludo's rule):
  - the decorator-chain walk exists four times beside core's `StateTree.getDecoratorRoot`;
  - the file-root walk ×7;
  - three `cssColor`s that disagree (the stage's rejects `#f80`) beside Prism UI's `parseColor`;
  - the "particle list as drawn" read ×6.
- **Traces that hold:**
  - Decorators under a moved list: `cryoet.offset` and the particle handle add their decorator at the end of the chain,
    so the preview's input is right.
  - A graded cut's records survive a move: they are keyed by the clip's tag, and `movedClipObject` keeps `tag` and
    `ghost`.
  - A mesh-triangle distance follows the mesh's move: its `dependsOn` is the mesh's representation (covered by
    `mesh-measure.test.ts`).
  - Theme by zoom on a cellPACK or PetWorld model: the mmCIF reader fills the per-particle `entities` and `compartments`
    it needs.
- **Not traced (GPU only):** GPU culling with ghosts.
