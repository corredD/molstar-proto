## Brief

**What Ludo sees.**

1. **Paint on a mesh.** With an OBJ, PLY or VTP mesh in the scene, a Paint stroke over it puts the copies on its
   surface (as Paint's surface placement does on particles), each turned to the triangle's normal (a membrane protein
   upright in it; others as Turn says). One undo step a stroke, as now.
2. **Grow › On: Mesh** (a select in the Grow section: None, or a mesh of the scene by its row's name; default: the mesh
   under the source when it was painted on one, else None). With a mesh:
   - **Tiling** grows over the surface, not a plane: ring by ring, each new point the frontier's neighbour at Spacing in
     the tangent plane, projected onto the surface (the closest triangle), its normal the triangle's; a point closer than
     0.8 × Spacing to one kept is dropped (TomoNet's rule), so a closed surface (a vesicle) fills without piling up.
     Rings is then a count limit (Count, default the plane's 1 + 3r(r+1)).
   - **Random walk** walks on the surface: each step's direction kept in the tangent plane, the point projected back.
   - **Sphere** puts its Count points on the surface within Radius of the source (geodesic not needed: points of the
     ball projected, or sampled on the triangles within Radius by area; the lane picks and says why).
   - **Crystal**: unchanged (a lattice is 3D), the select disabled with a hint.
   Each copy turned so its axis (the entity's, as Tiling uses now) follows the surface's normal; the in-plane spin as
   the source's (or random, Tiling's Turn if added: say).
3. Copies stay exactly where the rule says otherwise (overlaps between different grows allowed, as decided).

**Where it plugs in.**

- The surface: `getMeshTriangleLookup(mesh).pick(out, ray, near, tolerance)`
  (graphics/src/geo/geometry/mesh/triangle-pick.ts:41) gives the closest triangle to a point (a ray along the normal
  through it). The mesh's own space: apply its instance transform and the shape's scale (pixel size) both ways.
  Meshes are listed by `cryoet.meshes` (cryoet/src/operations.ts:260).
- Paint: `surfaceAt` (mesoscale/src/pick-surface.ts:66) and `PaintHost.refine` take particle loci today: add a shape's
  triangle loci.
- Grow: `tilingPoses`, `walkPath`, `spherePoses` (mesoscale/src/grow.ts:133, 184, 162) are pure: add a `surface`
  argument (a projector: point → { point, normal } | undefined) rather than a mesh, so the engine stays testable without
  the plugin. `mesoscale.grow` (mesoscale/src/operations.ts:1017) takes `mesh?` (a state ref or label) and builds the
  projector in the scene frame (before `toListFrame`, as now).
- The stroke's `grow` record keeps the mesh (its ref and label) for the report; the poses are explicit as now (the mesh
  can go and the copies stay).

## Tests (each first failing)

- The projector: a point off a unit sphere's mesh lands on it (|p| ≈ 1 within the mesh's chord error), its normal
  radial; a transformed and scaled mesh likewise in the scene frame.
- Tiling on a sphere mesh (radius 100 Å, spacing 10 Å): every copy on the surface, nearest neighbours ≈ spacing (no
  pair under 0.8 × spacing), each copy's axis along the normal, Count reached or the sphere full (about 4πr²/(√3/2·s²)
  × 0.9 at most).
- Tiling on a flat mesh equals the plane's tiling (the hex counts of round 1).
- Random walk on a sphere mesh: every point on the surface, steps a rise apart (± the chord), turns ≤ Persistence.
- Sphere on a mesh: Count points on the surface within Radius; the same seed, the same points.
- Paint over a mesh: the copy's center on the surface and its axis along the normal; one undo step.
- `mesoscale.grow` with `mesh`: refused for Crystal (with the reason) and for a mesh not in the scene; a session keeps
  the copies without the mesh.
