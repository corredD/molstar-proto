## Brief

**What Ludo sees.**

1. **Paint on a mesh.** With an OBJ, PLY or VTP mesh in the scene, a Paint stroke over it puts the copies on its surface
   (as Paint's surface placement does on particles), each turned to the triangle's normal (a membrane protein upright in
   it; others as Turn says). One undo step a stroke, as now.
2. **Grow › On: Mesh** (a select in the Grow section: None, or a mesh of the scene by its row's name; default: the mesh
   under the source when it was painted on one, else None). With a mesh:
   - **Tiling** grows over the surface, not a plane: ring by ring, each new point the frontier's neighbour at Spacing in
     the tangent plane, projected onto the surface (the closest triangle), its normal the triangle's; a point closer
     than 0.8 × Spacing to one kept is dropped (TomoNet's rule), so a closed surface (a vesicle) fills without piling
     up. Rings is then a count limit (Count, default the plane's 1 + 3r(r+1)).
   - **Random walk** walks on the surface: each step's direction kept in the tangent plane, the point projected back.
   - **Sphere** puts its Count points on the surface within Radius of the source (geodesic not needed: points of the
     ball projected, or sampled on the triangles within Radius by area; the lane picks and says why).
   - **Crystal**: unchanged (a lattice is 3D), the select disabled with a hint.

   Each copy turned so its axis (the entity's, as Tiling uses now) follows the surface's normal; the in-plane spin as
   the source's (or random, Tiling's Turn if added: say).

3. Copies stay exactly where the rule says otherwise (overlaps between different grows allowed, as decided).

**Where it plugs in.**

- The surface: `getMeshTriangleLookup(mesh).pick(out, ray, near, tolerance)`
  (graphics/src/geo/geometry/mesh/triangle-pick.ts:41) gives the closest triangle to a point (a ray along the normal
  through it). The mesh's own space: apply its instance transform and the shape's scale (pixel size) both ways. Meshes
  are listed by `cryoet.meshes` (cryoet/src/operations.ts:260).
- Paint: `surfaceAt` (mesoscale/src/pick-surface.ts:66) and `PaintHost.refine` take particle loci today: add a shape's
  triangle loci.
- Grow: `tilingPoses`, `walkPath`, `spherePoses` (mesoscale/src/grow.ts:133, 184, 162) are pure: add a `surface`
  argument (a projector: point → { point, normal } | undefined) rather than a mesh, so the engine stays testable without
  the plugin. `mesoscale.grow` (mesoscale/src/operations.ts:1017) takes `mesh?` (a state ref or label) and builds the
  projector in the scene frame (before `toListFrame`, as now).
- The stroke's `grow` record keeps the mesh (its ref and label) for the report; the poses are explicit as now (the mesh
  can go and the copies stay).

## Decided

- (author) **The projector**: a ray along a normal through the point (the parent's for Tiling, the walk's last), from a
  reach behind it, the hit nearest the point; else (no line, or no hit) the nearest point of the mesh within the reach.
  Its normal the vertex normals interpolated (a coarse sphere's are radial), turned to the side of the one before (a
  file's winding is no guide).
- (author) **The frontier rule (Tiling)**: breadth first from the source's point; each kept point's neighbours a Spacing
  away in its tangent plane (Hex 6 at 60°; Square 4 and the 4 diagonals at √2, so its rings are the plane's), projected;
  dropped when the projection moved it over half a Spacing (another sheet, an edge) or when it is under 0.8 × Spacing
  from one kept (TomoNet's rule; a hash grid of 0.8 s cells). The lattice's first direction carried from point to point
  in the tangent planes (the plane's at the source), so a flat mesh gives the plane's tiling exactly. Count: the points,
  the source's counted (unset: the plane's 1 + 3r(r+1) or (2r+1)²): a new setting (`tiles`), the op's `count` on a mesh,
  Rings hidden. **Not a Rings limit**: rings have no meaning on a vesicle.
- (author) **Sphere's sampling**: uniform by area on the triangles within Radius (pieces of a quarter Radius, those
  missing the ball dropped; a point outside the ball drawn again), not ball points projected: projection bunches points
  where the surface is nearest the ball's bulk and needs a reach of the Radius. The triangles' normals turned to one
  side by agreement with their neighbours from the one under the source (mixed winding, and a whole vesicle's far side,
  face out).
- (author) **The spin**: each copy is the source turned with its axis (+z for a membrane protein, else its principal
  axis) along the normal at the source, then by the least turn from that normal to its own: "as the source", carried. No
  Turn added for Tiling; Sphere's Turn Random spins about the normal. Near the antipode of a whole vesicle the least
  turn's axis is arbitrary (the axis right, the spin jumps there: open, NEW-1).
- (author) **The walk's frame**: on a mesh, walked in the scene (the projector's frame) with the rise times the list's
  scale (as drawn), then `toListFrame`: the same rise as round 1's. It ends where an open mesh ends (review M1).
- (author) The source within two diameters of the mesh, else refused; the mesh by state ref or file name; a mesh deleted
  after a grow leaves the copies (explicit poses).
- (author) A source's default On: the mesh it was painted on, else the file mesh within its radius of where it is drawn
  (review S7), else None. Crystal: a text "A crystal grows in space" in place of the select, not a disabled select (N1;
  the Select has no disabled state).
- (author) No dependency on cryoet (its `cryoet.meshes`): the meshes are found the same way from the state tree.

## Learned

- `canvas3d.getLoci(pickingId)` gives the group loci, not the triangle (only interaction-events' identify applies
  `getTriangleLoci`): paint refines by the mesh projector at the pick's point instead.
- `pick()`'s ray pass only hits ahead (t ≥ 0): the ray starts a reach behind the point; a zero direction meets no
  triangle and takes the nearest-point pass.
- The walk's projection always succeeds near an open edge (the edge is within a rise): a moved-by guard, not the
  projection's failure, ends it.
- A fiber's units are made every rise along the path: a walk whose rise is near the vesicle's radius sinks into it
  (chords); fine at rise ≪ radius.
- `mapState` over one state does not follow another read inside it: `derive([a, b], …)`.
