## Decided

- Coordinator (not Ludo), in the brief: `clipWhole` to stage-tools; "no direct call" means no write outside `api.run`
  (reads, previews and hosts stay typed methods); the picks to stage-tools as one read; the label sizing to stage-tools
  if it is logic; no `any` in `apps/prism-me/src` outside tests and stories.
- Lane: the label sizing is logic (a rule from two radii, not a default), so it moved; the `?? 4` shown in a label's
  properties is a display fallback and stays. The depth's rule moved with it (at a point clicked: in front by the
  height); the app still says "in front of the node's middle by its radius" (`depth`), because only it knows the point
  is the node's middle.
- Lane: the row a pick reveals is layout (its keys are the tree's, an entity of one chain has no chain rows), so the
  stage says what a click is on and the explorer makes the row. `pickedOf(loci)` kept its signature, so its tests prove
  the behaviour unchanged.
- Lane: `stage.clipWhole` writes the target representations itself, not through `particles.update` (its "already"
  differs) nor a Prism path: `particles.look` writes the Layers cell, `clipping.settings`' variant is the scene's and
  has no `clipPrimitive`.
- Lane: no agent skill or ⌘K command for `stage.clipWhole` (the app's op had none); its title is its command if added.

## Learned

- TypeScript here is 7 (`tsgo`): no JS API to walk a file's types from a test, so the no-`any` check blanks comments and
  strings and looks for the word.
- A label's and a distance's shapes need a canvas for their text: headless, their representation has no object unless a
  test sets the font stand-in (`setCanvasModule`), as `distance.test.ts` does.
- The stage's structure pick (`structureRefsOf`: every cell, twice) and the app's (up from `substructureParent`) found
  the same structure on every test; the stage's is O(cells) a click (not a hot path), as its menu pick already was.
- The label's object radius is now the stage's (its target's bounds: the structure's boundary, the drawn list's) where
  the app used its node's loci bounds; equal for crambin (the pinned sizes), maybe a little different for a list (its
  particles' bounding sphere vs their loci's).
