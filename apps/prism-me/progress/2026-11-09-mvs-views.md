## Built

Steps 1-4. Steps 5 (the app check) and 6 (the gate) are the coordinator's.

- **Step 1, a view shown is no step.**
  - The runtime: `updateView(doc, ir, view?)`, `compile`'s `view` option, and `shownViewOf`. The view shown is the one
    picked, else `session.view`, else the first.
  - `MesoSession.view` is passed to every derivation: an edit, a stream step and a restore. `show(view)` runs in the
    edit queue. `restoreView(view)` handles the same document with another view.
  - `MesoDocumentModel.show` draws through the run's `api`. It makes the saved value again with `shown`, and holds no
    keys.
  - `restore` derives only the view when the document is the same, then adopts the restored tree. A view an edit removes
    is no longer picked.
  - `mesoviewspec.view` is `undoable: false` and quiet, and is in the stub.
  - The eye, Show only and the style looks go to the view shown.
- **Step 2, the Views row and tours.**
  - The tree state gains `views`: the views and tours, the view shown, the view it opens with, and the tour playing. It
    is the same object while those are the same.
  - `mesoviewspec.tour.play` (`history: false`, `undoable: false`) and `.tour.stop`. Each step is the caller's
    `mesoviewspec.view`; its caption goes in the state.
  - A tour stops on Stop, another tour, Undo or Redo, a close or an open. A step whose view is gone is skipped.
  - prism-me: the Views row is last under the document. A view's row says "shown" or "opens with". A tour's row says "2
    of 3" with its caption as the tip.
  - A click on a view shows it: `TreeView.onClick`, so a key's move only picks. A double click shows a view or plays a
    tour. The ⋯ has Show this view, and Play or Stop.
- **Step 3, new view, camera, open with.**
  - `mesoviewspec.newView` makes `v.view.<n>`: the camera here, the shown view's looks with new ids (each look keeps its
    kind), shown in the same step.
  - `.updateCamera` sets the camera of the view shown. `.openWith` sets `session.view`; the view shown is pinned first.
  - The camera comes from the canvas or a `camera` param (Å). It is written in the view's frame (the frame's matrix
    inverted) or in the document's unit.
  - In the ⋯ of the Views row and of a view.
- **Step 4, annotations.**
  - The adapter draws the view's annotations through `stage.label`, inside the run, sized at a 40th of the scene radius
    and marked by the node `md:#an:<id>`. Labels of annotations that are gone or changed are deleted.
  - `adopt` rebuilds the labels from the restored tree; `clear` deletes them.
  - `mesoviewspec.label` makes an annotation `an.<n>` of the view shown. A document row's ⋯ › Add a label.

## Decided

- **Undo leaves the camera alone.** Undo shows the restored view's looks (the tree holds them), but checkpoints never
  hold the camera (sequence.md §7.2: view state), so the camera stays where it is. The other way: move the camera on a
  restore.
- **A removed view.** A view an edit removes is no longer picked, so the document's own view is shown. Undo shows the
  removed view again: its checkpoint noted it.
- **Operation ids**, not in §5.3's table: `mesoviewspec.newView`, `.updateCamera`, `.openWith`, `.label`. Tours are
  `.tour.play { tour? }` and `.tour.stop`. With no tour named, `.tour.play` plays `session.tour`, else the first tour.
- **New view.** Its id is `v.view.<n>` and its label `View <n>`. It is shown in the same step, so the next checkpoint
  notes it. Copied looks keep their kind prefix (`l.style.`, `l.eye.`, `l.only.`); an author's look becomes
  `l.copy.<n>`. The view's selection, annotations and cutaway are not copied (§4.4 says looks).
- **Open with this view keeps the view shown.** When no view was picked, it pins the view shown first, so the screen
  does not change.
- **A tour step** with no duration holds 2 s (the semantic-camera story's value).
- **The caption is not in the canvas.** It is the tour row's tip, because Prism has no canvas caption line (the snapshot
  card is the snapshots feature's own).
- **Labels are made through `stage.label` by operation id.** That needs no import of stage-tools. Their size is a 40th
  of the scene radius, at least 4 Å; Prism's default of 4 Å is invisible at a cell's scale.
- **A view's row:** a click shows it, and a key's move only picks it. A tour plays from its ⋯ or a double click, never
  from a single click.
- **Showing a view writes the custom state again with `shown`.** The next checkpoint's size pass measures the document
  once more. This is cheap, but it is not free.

## Open (for the gate and Ludo)

- **A label's row, its text, its removal.** An annotation's label is not a tree row. Its node is not a known key, so
  rows.ts does not list it. `stage.editLabel` on it changes the label only, not the document: it needs a hook in
  stage-tools, outside this lane's files (as `particles.remove` in mvs-drive). The way to remove one now is a patch
  (`mesoviewspec.edit`).
- **The caption line (Ludo):** where a tour's caption should show in the canvas.
- **A failed show is not rolled back.** If `mesoviewspec.view` fails mid-draw, it leaves what it drew: an
  `undoable: false` run has no checkpoint. The session keeps its view.
- **What Undo does to the camera** (see Decided), for Ludo to confirm.
