## Built

- **The eyes and the ⋯** (`tree-view.tsx`, shared with the mock): every row's eye and ⋯ always in view, each in a column
  of its own (a row without one keeps its room); the brush, the scissors and the trash moved into the ⋯ as Color…, Add
  clip… and Remove (`menu.ts`, `ui.tsx`), a category's ⋯ its Color… (its themes; Uniform… at the ⋯) and Add clip… (every
  object in it), a clip row's ⋯ its Remove; no Hide in any menu (a distance's neither). The color dot is a button:
  Color…'s items.
- **A parent's eye** (`rows.ts` `setEyes`): every row under it in one `api.group` (a model: `particles.visibility` and
  one `mesoscale.style` of all its entities; a list: its targets in one `particles.look`; a category: one
  `state.visibility` of its objects, then each one's parts; a volume: Mol\*'s subtree already); a shown part draws its
  object again. Read back: an object's eye open when it and all its parts are shown, a group's when all its entities
  are, a category's when all its objects' are; a category's Look › Visible the same.
- **The drag** (`eyeDrag`): Prism's `drag()` on the tree; the press previews the first row and the rows under it in the
  eye states (`shown`), each move the rows passed (and the rows between, by index, on a fast move), the release calls

## Decided

- **A parent's eye state** (coordinator's default within Ludo's words): stored in the scene, so Undo brings it back: an
  object's is its own flag (cell visibility) and all its parts; a group's and a category's, all under it. So "hide the
  model, then show one group alone" leaves the model's eye closed (as the mock), and hiding one entity closes its
  parents' eyes (the mock keeps them: it holds a flag per row with nothing under it). A volume's representations are not
  its parts: a tomogram's hidden slices leave its eye open.
- **What a parent's eye sets**: the scene's parts (entities, targets, representations, objects), not the labels,
  distances and clips under the row (they keep their own eyes; TODO.md backlog, Ludo may want them).
- **Its compartment**: cellPACK's path `root.<compartment>.(surface|interior).<group>` cut before its last surface or
  interior, else its parent (a membrane beside the surface); entities with the same key. Its group: the group it is
  directly in, as the tree groups the model.
- **Show only on a model's entities** writes all of them hidden (or shown) in one `mesoscale.style` with no names, then
  the kept ones by name: no per-name lookup over all entities (`selectEntities` finds each name by a scan).
- **The menu's order** follows the mock: Focus, Move, Color…, Add label, Add clip…, Show only…, Remove. Remove is
  "Remove" (the trash's word), on an object, a label, a distance and a clip.
- **A structure's entity and chain have no eye** (Prism hides whole representations): their column is empty (the mock
  shows eyes there; TODO.md backlog).

## Learned

- The app's tree is in a shadow root: `document.elementFromPoint` answers the host. The drag reads the point in the
  tree's root node, taken at the press (the element is not in the page when the tree is built).
- `particles.look` fails on a list with no representation: a parent's eye skips the parts of an undrawn list (else the
  group fails and rolls the whole step back).
- A group (`api.group`) fails as a whole when any run inside failed: an "already so" run is fine (ok, unchanged).
