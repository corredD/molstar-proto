Grow › Symmetry: copies of the source by a point group about a center.

- Groups: Cn (n 2–60), Dn, T (12), O (24), I (60). Helical is Grow › Helix; a space group is a structure built with its
  symmetry and painted (Ludo, 2026-11-13), so neither is here.
- The center: a Radius from the source along its axis (paint's axis rule: Fiber Axis, else +z; membrane +z), toward the
  inside (Side flips it); the group's principal axis through the center along a chosen direction (Auto: the source's
  axis turned so the source sits on the shell; View; X/Y/Z). I with the source on a 5-fold-free general position (60
  copies, no overlap of the source with itself).
- The source is copy 0 (not placed again); copies are the source's pose by each operator (exact orientations).
- On a mesh: optional "snap": each copy projected onto the mesh and turned to its normal (off by default, since a
  symmetry is exact).

## Decided

- (author) **The group's frame**: its principal axis z is Cn's and Dn's n-fold, O's 4-fold, T's and I's 2-fold (the
  standard setting: T's and I's 2-folds on x, y, z; I's 5-folds through (0, ±1, ±φ)).
- (author) **The general position** (the brief's "5-fold-free general position" for I, made general for every group):
  the normalized sum of the directions of a triangle of adjacent axes, which lies on none: I, its 2-, 3- and 5-fold (z,
  the face (0, ±1, φ), (φ, 0, 1), and (0, 1, φ)); O and T, O's 4-, 3- and 2-fold (for T, still on none of its axes); Dn,
  z and the 2-folds at 0 and π/n (about 27° off the equator: D7 is a barrel of two rings of 7); Cn, x (the ring's
  equator).
- (author) **Auto**: the source at the general position; the rest of the frame from the source's own (its side,
  `fiberOffsetDirection` of its axis, turned by it), so the assembly turns with the source (tested: equivariance).
  **View, X, Y, Z**: the principal axis set, the source where it is, its azimuth about the axis the general position's;
  refused when two copies would coincide (the source on an axis of the group), "Another Axis, or Auto".
- (author) **Side**: Inside (default) puts the center a Radius against the source's axis (a subunit pointing out of its
  shell), Outside along it. On a mesh the axis follows the mesh's normals as painted, so a file wound inward flips which
  side is the inside (Side flips it back).
- (author) **Radius unset**: the copies touching (their nearest two a copy's diameter apart), not the copy's diameter as
  the helix's: a C60 or an I at a diameter would pile up. The value is recorded with the grow (as every mode's), so
  after a Grow, n changes keep the center where it is.
- (author) **Snap**: each copy projected along its line from the center within the grow's reach (two diameters), its
  axis turned to the normal on its side (as `sourceOn`); a copy with no surface within reach stays where the group puts
  it (the count stays |G| − 1). Snap is recorded as `snap: 1`; a mesh chosen with Snap off grows in space (the mesh
  recorded, as On shows it).
- (author) **Not prefix-stable**: a symmetry is a set (as Crystal's copies' order): n changes every copy.
- (author) **Keys of its own**: `symmetryRadius` (Sphere's `radius`, Helix's `helixRadius` mean other things),
  `symmetryAxis` (`axis` is dropped by `growParams` in space), `n` (not Count: Sphere's least is 1, Spiral's 2).
