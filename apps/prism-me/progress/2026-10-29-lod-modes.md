# The graphics modes change the levels of detail (2026-10-29, lane `lod-modes`)

## Brief

- **Asked** (Ludo, TODO.md `Next` › From the cellVIEW evaluation, item 1): Performance, Balanced, Quality, Ultra write
  the pixel presets (`Spheres.LodPixelPresets[mode]`: levels and `lodPixelSize`), not the distance presets the particles
  ignore at 3 px; the mode read back compares both. **Check:** on a cellPACK model, Performance draws visibly fewer
  spheres far away than Ultra, and the mode picked stays lit.
- **Spec:** [../\_spec/ui.md](../_spec/ui.md) §4 (Graphics & Motion and its table); found in
  [2026-10-28-cellview-evaluation.md](2026-10-28-cellview-evaluation.md) › Found on the way.
- **What is wrong:** `withMode` (`mesoscale/src/operations.ts`) writes `Spheres.LodLevelsPresets[mode]` (distances) to
  `lodLevels` and leaves `lodPixelSize` as it is. A particle list draws by size on screen at 3 px by default, so its
  distances are ignored: a mode changes the strides, not where they start. A cellPACK structure from the mesoscale
  preset has the same pixel LOD. The mode read (`graphicsOf`) compares the levels only, against the distance presets.
- **Tests (first, red):** for each mode, a particle list's `lodLevels` and `lodPixelSize` are its pixel preset's, and
  the mode read lights that mode; the preset's levels at another pixel size read as no mode.
- **Touches:** `packages/extensions/mesoscale/src/operations.ts` (`withMode`, `graphicsOf`), its test
  `object-graphics.test.ts`, ui.md §4, features.md, TODO.md (the item removed), `.v6/changelogs/lod-modes.md`.
- **Size:** ~15 production lines; stop over 150 or if Prism core must change.
- **Steps:** 1. this brief 2. the test, red 3. the fix and the spec, one commit 4. the gate 5. review gate 6. real GL on
  WebGPU and WebGL2 (port 1351) 7. PR.

## Built

- `withMode` writes `Spheres.LodPixelPresets[mode]`: the levels and `lodPixelSize` (Ultra 1.5 px, Quality 3, Balanced 4,
  Performance 6), wherever a representation has `lodLevels` (a particle list's spacefill and target, a structure's
  spacefill), `lodPixelSize` where it has one.
- `graphicsOf` reads a particle list's mode by both (`isLodPreset`), and only on the representations that have levels of
  detail: a cellPACK list's `fibers` representation has none and made every list read as mixed, so no mode was ever lit
  on the real HIV (found in real GL, not in the brief; its own commit and test, a `TestFiberList` fixture).
- ui.md §4 (the table gives the pixel size per mode; the read), features.md, TODO.md (item 1 removed), changelog.

## Decided

- Structures too: a cellPACK structure from the mesoscale preset is pixel LOD as well
  (`representation-presets/ mesoscale.ts`), and ui.md's table says "particles, spacefill". A plain structure's spacefill
  got the distance presets before, now the pixel ones.
- The read compares the levels and the pixel size exactly; a list whose levels are another (a session saved with the
  distance presets, a hand edit) lights no mode, as a mixed one.
- Out of scope, left as is: the mesoscale preset's per-mode `approximate` and `alphaThickness`; the motion read still
  spans every representation (a fibers one has no `animation`, so it reads 0).

## Learned

- The new test failed first on the old code (`lodLevels` were the distance preset); the fibers test failed first with
  `mode` undefined.
- The unit fixture's spacefill starts at `lodLevels: []`, `lodPixelSize: 0`; the app's cellPACK list's `target` starts
  at Quality's pixel preset and its `spacefill` at `[]`/0: a fresh list is mixed and lights no mode.
- Real GL (Playwright, headless Chrome, `?example=cellpack-hiv1`, port 1351), the list's mode by `mesoscale.graphics`
  and by the Graphics & Motion buttons, default camera:
  - WebGPU: Ultra fine atoms, Performance large coarse spheres; edge energy (sum of luminance gradients over the
    viewport) 9.19M Ultra, 5.94M Performance. WebGL2: 10.25M and 6.35M. Ultra and Performance lit after each; Balanced
    clicked in the panel lights Balanced.
  - The old code on WebGPU: no button lit after any mode, and Performance had more edge energy than Ultra (10.65M and
    8.67M): broken-looking sparse spheres, not coarser ones.
  - Four times farther both modes draw the same picture: every instance is at the last level in both.
