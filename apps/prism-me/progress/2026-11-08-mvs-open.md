## Decided

- The drop route is a Prism addition (`owns.opens`), not in the brief's Touches: `load.files` hard-coded SeqViewSpec,
  and a stub's sniff only loads the extension (ours, the coordinator to confirm upstream).
- A drop holding a document hands it every other file as its resources; those it does not name are said (ours).
- A relative `?mesoviewspec=` is resolved against the page; `fixture://x` is `fixtures/x` beside the document, the CLI's
  rule (ours).
- The custom state's revision is the document's `revision`; the owner gives each committed patch `r.<uuid>` (ours).
- An empty placement set draws no list (spacefill refuses an empty list, which failed the open) (ours).

## Learned

- mol-state never applies a transform again for equal params: a fill after a cold restore needs a param that changes
  (`fill`).
- The committed `exosome.mesoviewspec.json` names its recipe `examples/fixtures/exosome.cellpack-gpu.json`, relative to
  `packages/mesoscale/spec/src`, not to the example's folder: by URL only the lipids and the picks draw (the recipe is a
  404). Dropped with `exosome.cellpack-gpu.json`, `catalase_picks.star` and `membrane.obj`, all of it draws.
- In the app the Particles category shows one row per list (3), not one per document: that is lane `mvs-tree`.
