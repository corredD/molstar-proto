Prism findings (primitives the design wants that prism-ui does not export): the scene tree (`SceneTree`, grouped and
virtualized since #2043, with swatch, eye, ⋯, search and `TreeNavigation`) is the viewer's, private and bound to its
`SceneModel`; `SceneRow` (exported) fits only a nested tree, not a flat virtual one (no swatch, no ⋯). So the mock
repeats the tree's row rules in its own CSS (`explorer.module.css`, cited). Also: no exported splitter (the dock's);
`installTooltips` not in the package index; a row's ⋯ menu in Prism's tree is a `MenuPopover` (exported, anchored,
raised) filled by `MenuItems`, which is the viewer's own, so the mock opens `openContextMenu` under the ⋯ instead (no
sub-menus: Add clip… opens a second menu at the same place, as the app does). `openContextMenu`'s surface is the glass
panel tone: opened over the tree, the rows show through it (Right-click stories); Prism's ⋯ popover is raised and does
not.

**Refactor in place or prism-me-v2.** The mocks measure the view layer only. There, the gap is in the containers and
their rules, not in what is shown: every ui.md place maps one to one onto today's rows, sections and menus (10 of the
table's 27 rows the same, one missing; most of the rest differ in how, not what), and most controls inside the sections
are already Prism's (`ParamsForm`, `Select`, `Choices`, `Slider`, `ColorPicker`; a mesh's color is still a native
input). What differs is the tree (not virtualized, rows rebuilt and compared as JSON, inline off-scale styles,
always-shown actions, its own keys and frame calls, a filter that drops a hit's parents), the folding sections, the
split, the wording of + Open and the empty line, and three spec gaps (clips as rows, a Labels section, the menu's extra
items). That is a rewrite of `ui.tsx` (673 lines) and a restyle of all of `properties.tsx` (1,203 lines: its `Section`,
`ColorField` and header, and its 57 inline `style=` lines on the `Line` and `Label` constants, 28 and 64 px), with the
extensions' controls restyled the same way (cryoet's 38 inline styles, its native color input). `rows.ts` keeps its
flattening (moved onto `flattenTree`, keyed), `menu.ts` its items, but not its handlers: `addClip`, `addLabel`,
`removeObject`, `showHandles` and the brush's `paint` call the extensions directly and move into operations by
architecture.md §7 decision 5, whichever path. So refactor in place, view file by view file, each against its story; v2
buys nothing the data layer needs, since decisions 1–5 hold either way, and the view work above is the same size in a
new app. What costs on either path: Prism's tree is not exported. Either Prism exports `SceneTree` over a `SceneModel`
the explorer can implement (an upstream change, the better end: the explorer's nodes in Prism's own tree, search and
selection filter included), or the explorer keeps its own rows on `VirtualList` as the mock does, repeating the tree's
CSS.

## Decided

- The mocks follow Prism where ui.md and Prism differ, and flag it here (ui.md unchanged, per the brief): the eye sits
  at the row's end with the ⋯, shown on hover and kept while hidden (Prism's tree, styling.md §4), where ui.md §3 lists
  the eye first. To confirm (Ludovic).
- A row's ⋯ opens the same menu as its right-click (Prism's tree has one); ui.md §3 does not list a ⋯. The app's brush
  and scissors on the row are not mocked: Color… and Add clip are in the menu (§5).
- ui.md §4's Clips (shape, invert, position, rotation, size per clip) is mocked as written; the app's Status
  (2026-10-22) says shape and side are Prism's handles' and not repeated. One of the two should change (Ludovic).
- Properties stories open every section to show it; Layout keeps them folded, as the app starts.
- Only `Look` has a visible toggle besides the eye; ui.md §4 lists "visible" under Look.
- Touched beyond the brief's list, for the wiring: `storybook/.storybook/preview.ts` (the sidebar order),
  `apps/prism-me/tsconfig.storybook.json` (the stories' typecheck), `pnpm-lock.yaml` (the Storybook devDependency),
  `apps/prism-me/TODO.md` (two open items from the review).

## Learned

- prism-ui's `SectionList` works outside the shell: a `new ShellModel()`, `registerSections` with `when` on the subject,
  `subject` the picked node. It gives the folding, the reveal, the pause and the open state per slot for free.
- `TreeNavigation` and `VirtualList` + `flattenTree` + `keepRows` make a keyboard tree in ~150 lines; Prism's
  `SceneTree` is the same over its `SceneModel`.
- A context menu opened inside a `contain: paint` panel is clipped (its `position: fixed` is relative to the container):
  open it at the app's root, as the app does.
- `apps/prism-me/tsconfig.json` excludes `_stories`: the stories are checked by `typecheck:storybook` only, which CI
  does not run for apps (`.github/workflows/node.yml` runs it for `packages/prism/*`).
