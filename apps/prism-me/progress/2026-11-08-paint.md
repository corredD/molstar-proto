Ludo's vision (2026-11-07): pack (cellPACK), paint (cellPAINT / cellPAINT 3D-VR), load (cif, .star, segmentations) in

## Design

1. **Data: a `ParticleListPainted` decorator** (the mesoscale extension, per Decided 1; modelled on plugin core's
   `ParticleListOffsets`): params
   `{ strokes: [{ target, entity, compartment, positions: number[], rotations: number[] }] }`; it appends each stroke's
   particles to its input list (new keys after the last; targets, entities, compartments extended; `targetMapping`
   unchanged since a painted copy reuses an existing target). In the state tree, so a session saves and reloads it and
   undo removes a stroke.
2. **Operation `mesoscale.paint`** `{ list, target, positions, normals, spin? }` → one commit adding one stroke to the
   list's `ParticleListPainted` (created on first use). One history step per stroke. Orientation: the target's local +z
   along the normal, a random spin about it (the convention mesobuild's membrane targets use: their mid-plane at z = 0);
   for any other target, its principal axis (documented, a param to override).
3. **Gesture: "Paint with …"** in the right-click menu of a particle entity row (or a particle in the canvas): enters a
   paint mode with that entity's target as the brush; Esc or a second click on the item leaves it. A stroke is a
   `drag()` on a canvas overlay: each frame (`frameState`) one `identify` at the pointer and three at ±2 px give the
   position and a normal (the cross product of the neighbours' offsets: works on any representation); points closer than
   the brush spacing to the stroke's last are skipped. Preview: small oriented discs drawn by a transient shape outside
   the state (no state change while drawing); release commits `mesoscale.paint` once.
4. **Properties** show the brush (target, spacing in Å, spin random/fixed, side: along or against the normal) while the
   mode is on.

## v2 (not in this brief): fibres

A stroke becomes a new fibre (the list's `fibers` CSR gains one row of the stroke's points at the fibre's spacing),
drawn by the fibers representation; edit = drag a point (setParticleFrames already moves existing ones); wrap = project
the stroke on a picked object's surface offset by the fibre radius.

## Built

- `ParticleListPainted` (packages/extensions/mesoscale/src/paint.ts): a decorator that appends each stroke's copies to
  the list. Keys go after the largest. Targets, entities, compartments, orientations and radii are extended. Attributes
  read as not present for the copies. Fibres and `targetMapping` are kept. It is registered, so sessions keep it (a
  state-snapshot test). The copies' orientations come from `paintRotations`, with a seed, stored in the params so redo
  and reload are exact.
- `mesoscale.paint` (operations.ts): one stroke per run, one commit, one history step. The first stroke inserts the
  decorator right under the list, with the list's Transform (identity) under it, so a later `stage.move` or
  `stage.scale` carries the copies. Points and normals are mapped into the list's frame. The orientation defaults by
  compartment: a membrane or surface compartment gives +z along the normal, anything else gets a random orientation.
  `axis`, `view`, `side`, `spin` and `seed` are options. Lists whose targets come from a class are refused. The
  operation has a command and an agent tool, `paint_mesoscale_copies`.
- `surfaceAt` (pick-surface.ts): the point and normal under the pointer, from the picks 4 px left, right, up and down.
  It takes the central difference where both sides hit the same surface, else the shorter side, else faces the viewer.
- Paint mode (paint-mode.ts, paint-host.ts):
  - A layer over the canvas in its own scope. The stroke is a `drag()`. Once a frame, the path since the last read is
    sampled every 4 px. A copy goes every spacing (default: a copy's diameter).
  - The preview is discs lying on the surface, drawn on a 2D layer. Nothing is allocated per pointer event.
  - Release commits the stroke. Esc stops the mode, an undo while drawing cancels the stroke, and removing the list or
    disposing the viewer ends the mode. A refused stroke is reported on the status line.
- UI:
  - An entity's right-click: Paint with … / Stop painting (menu.ts).
  - A Paint section in the entity's properties (controls.tsx `PaintBrush`, properties.tsx): Paint/Stop, Spacing, Turn,
    Side, Spin.
- Size: about 1,190 production lines, of which about 920 are code without comments, against the brief's 600–800. The
  extra room went to:
  - the paint mode, about 300 lines: way sampling, pending picks, preview math, lifecycle;
  - the operation's frame mapping and validation;
  - the review's fixes.

## Decided

- Ludo (2026-11-08): the brief's three decisions:
  1. The code lives in the mesoscale extension.
  2. Copies get a random orientation off a membrane; axis and view are options.
  3. No collisions.
- Lane (to confirm with Ludo):
  - On a membrane particle, a membrane protein's brush takes that particle's frame: its +z as the normal, and the point
    moved onto its mid-plane. This deviates from the brief's pure cross product. Why: the pick hits the outer leaflet's
    atoms, so copies would float about 2 nm out, and a normal taken over lipids is noisy. Other brushes keep the picked
    point.
  - The picks for a normal are 4 px apart, not 2. Why: the pick buffer is half resolution.
  - The preview is a 2D layer of projected discs, not a transient 3D shape. Why: a shape in the scene marks the pick
    dirty, and the pick pass would be redrawn over 88k–2M particles at each accepted point.
  - The brush's settings belong to the mode, not to the state (no history step).
  - The Dawn (headless WebGPU) normal test was dropped. Its first picks did not answer reliably here: no hit after a
    camera fit, or sub-Å pixels where the depth precision dominates. The normal is tested on a fake pick of an analytic
    sphere. The real check is the app's (TODO).

## Round 2 (Ludo, 2026-11-09): the camera while painting, a copy at the pointer, soluble and fibre brushes

**Asked (Ludo, after trying v1 on 1352).** "In paint mode we can't control the camera anymore, need a modifier [...]
Alt+ will make the camera mouse behave like in normal mode. Paint need to be able to draw soluble protein at the tip of
the mouse, the wheel control how far in Z behind the mouse. In paint mode, the painted object has a copy at the end of
the mouse, and when we click we create the instance. [...] paint also fiber [...] and soluble. All of that was done in
cellPaintVR."

**Decided (Ludo, 2026-11-09, asked).**

1. Camera: Alt + the usual gestures while painting (Alt+left drag turns, Alt+right drag pans, Alt+wheel zooms). Paint
   mode swaps the trackball's bindings for their Alt versions on start and restores them on stop; the layer lets an Alt
   press through to the canvas. The plain wheel sets the soluble depth.
2. Soluble depth: from the camera's focus plane (its target's distance) at the mode's start; the wheel moves it along
   the pointer's ray, in or out; shown in the hint in Å. Over empty space too.
3. The kind of brush is automatic, with toggles to override it: a membrane ingredient goes on the surface (v1), a soluble
   one at the pointer's depth, a fibre ingredient (its particles in the list's `fibers`) makes a fibre. The Paint section
   gets Place: Surface | Depth and a Fibre switch.
4. A fibre stroke makes a new fibre: the way resampled every segment (the entity's own fibre spacing, else a copy's
   diameter) becomes a new polyline of the list's `fibers` and its points copies of the ingredient, each turned along
   the tangent. One stroke, one step.

**Design.**

- A copy at the pointer: one copy of the brush drawn in 3D, a representation outside the state (the particle
  representation's own provider and the list's representation props, on a one-particle list of the brush's target), not
  pickable, moved by its transform once a frame (no rebuild). It follows the pointer in every place: on the surface
  (oriented as the copy will be), at the depth, and at a fibre's head. A click paints one copy, a drag a stroke (v1).
  The stroke's trail stays the 2D layer (discs; a fibre's polyline).
- Cost: any draw marks the pick stale (`canvas3d.ts`, `pickHelper.dirty`), so on a surface each frame the copy moves
  redraws the pick once. Measured on MG before it is kept (WebGPU, still camera, pointer moving); at depth no pick is
  read. If too costly: the copy moves on the surface only when the pointer rests one frame.
- Data: `ParticleListPainted`'s stroke gains `fiber?: boolean`; a fibre stroke's copies are appended and one polyline
  added to `fibers` (offsets, indices: the copies' new indices), the list's existing fibres kept. `mesoscale.paint` gains
  `place: 'surface' | 'depth'` (only how normals are read: at depth, no normal, a random turn or the tangent) and
  `fiber`.

**Tests (each failing first).** The bindings swapped on start and restored on stop, Esc and the list's removal; an Alt

session keeps it; the copy at the pointer follows the pointer, is not pickable, is gone on stop; the kind chosen by
compartment and by fibre membership.
