# Lane paint (2026-11-08): Paint v1

Brief approved by Ludo on 2026-11-08 ("yes" to the three recommendations below). Lane `me/paint` from `origin/prism-me`,
port 1352.

Ludo's vision (2026-11-07): pack (cellPACK), paint (cellPAINT / cellPAINT 3D-VR), load (cif, .star, segmentations) in
one scene; painting membrane proteins and fibres, wrapping and editing fibres. Load works; painting is the gap. This
brief is v1: paint copies of an ingredient onto a surface. Fibres are v2 (below).

## What exists to build on (survey of `me/meso-showcase`, file:line)

- `ParticleList` is immutable (`packages/model/src/model/particles/particle-list.ts:57-147`); every change is a new list
  `{...list, …}`. No operation appends or removes particles today; decorators change existing ones
  (`ParticleListOffsets`, `ParticleListTransform`:
  `packages/plugin/core/src/state/transforms/particles/ops.ts:448-615`).
- Drag preview without state: `setParticleFrames`
  (`packages/graphics/src/repr/particles/representation/target/representation.ts:239`) rewrites existing instances only:
  it cannot show new ones.
- Gestures: Prism's `drag()` (`packages/prism/ui/src/dom/scoped.ts:186-216`) and the canvas-overlay pattern of
  `startMoveDrag` (`packages/extensions/stage-tools/src/drag.ts:20-78`): preview on move, one operation on release.
- Picking gives a world position, no normal: `Canvas3D.identify` → `PickData {id, position}`
  (`packages/graphics/src/canvas3d/canvas3d.ts:1730`, `passes/pick.ts:28`).
- An operation is one history step (`apps/prism-me/_spec/architecture.md:24, 100-110`); the shape to copy is
  `stage.scale` (`packages/extensions/stage-tools/src/operations.ts:413-451`) with its tests
  (`_test/operations.test.ts`).
- UI goes in the explorer's four places (`apps/prism-me/_spec/ui.md:3-5`); the right-click menu is built in
  `packages/extensions/stage-tools/src/menu.ts`.

## Design

1. **Data: a `ParticleListPainted` decorator** (the mesoscale extension, per Decided 1; modelled on plugin core's
   `ParticleListOffsets`): params
   `{ strokes: [{ target, entity, compartment, positions: number[], rotations: number[] }] }`; it appends each stroke's
   particles to its input list (new keys after the last; targets, entities, compartments extended; `targetMapping`
   unchanged since a painted copy reuses an existing target). In the state tree, so a session saves and reloads it and
   undo removes a stroke.
2. **Operation `mesoscale.paint`** `{ list, target, positions, normals, spin? }` → one commit adding one stroke to the
   list's `ParticleListPainted` (created on first use). One history step per stroke. Orientation: the target's local +z
   along the normal, a random spin about it (the convention mesobuild's membrane targets use: their mid-plane at z = 0);
   for any other target, its principal axis (documented, a param to override).
3. **Gesture: "Paint with …"** in the right-click menu of a particle entity row (or a particle in the canvas): enters a
   paint mode with that entity's target as the brush; Esc or a second click on the item leaves it. A stroke is a
   `drag()` on a canvas overlay: each frame (`frameState`) one `identify` at the pointer and three at ±2 px give the
   position and a normal (the cross product of the neighbours' offsets: works on any representation); points closer than
   the brush spacing to the stroke's last are skipped. Preview: small oriented discs drawn by a transient shape outside
   the state (no state change while drawing); release commits `mesoscale.paint` once.
4. **Properties** show the brush (target, spacing in Å, spin random/fixed, side: along or against the normal) while the
   mode is on.

## v2 (not in this brief): fibres

A stroke becomes a new fibre (the list's `fibers` CSR gains one row of the stroke's points at the fibre's spacing),
drawn by the fibers representation; edit = drag a point (setParticleFrames already moves existing ones); wrap = project
the stroke on a picked object's surface offset by the fibre radius.

## Tests (each failing first where it guards a bug)

- Decorator: appends, keys unique and after the input's, targets / entities / compartments extended, fibres untouched;
  an empty stroke list returns the input list itself.
- Operation: one history step (undo removes exactly the stroke; redo restores it); a second stroke appends.
- Gesture: `lostReleases` (Prism `testing/gestures.ts`): no listener leak, a lost release commits nothing.
- Pick normal: headless (Dawn): identify on a sphere's spacefill at the centre pixel gives a normal within 10° of the
  analytic one.
- Scaling guard (`*.scaling.ts`): a stroke of n and 4n points, time and allocation ≈ linear.

## What Ludo checks in the app

Load `exosome_plasma.bcif` (mesobuild); right-click a CD9 copy → Paint with CD9; drag across the membrane: discs follow
the pointer, oriented out; release: the copies appear, upright in the bilayer; Ctrl-Z removes the stroke; save and
reload the session: the stroke is there.

## Size and models

About 600–800 production lines (decorator ~150, operation ~120, gesture + preview ~250, menu/properties ~100, normal
helper ~60) plus tests. Design and the gesture's hot path: Opus; the decorator's tests, the menu wiring, running the
checks: Sonnet. Review gate (reviewer + skeptic) before the PR.

## Decided (Ludo, 2026-11-08)

1. The decorator and the operation live in the mesoscale extension (`packages/extensions/mesoscale`), prism-me only; a
   move to plugin core is a later lane once it has proved itself.
2. A non-membrane brush gives each copy a random orientation; principal axis and view direction are brush options.
3. No collision checks in v1 (copies may overlap, as in cellPAINT 2D); rejection is v2 (mesobuild's collision code).
