Brief approved by Ludo on 2026-11-08 ("yes" to the three recommendations below). Lane `me/paint` from `origin/prism-me`,
port 1352.

Ludo's vision (2026-11-07): pack (cellPACK), paint (cellPAINT / cellPAINT 3D-VR), load (cif, .star, segmentations) in

## Design

1. **Data: a `ParticleListPainted` decorator** (the mesoscale extension, per Decided 1; modelled on plugin core's
   `ParticleListOffsets`): params
   `{ strokes: [{ target, entity, compartment, positions: number[], rotations: number[] }] }`; it appends each stroke's
   particles to its input list (new keys after the last; targets, entities, compartments extended; `targetMapping`
   unchanged since a painted copy reuses an existing target). In the state tree, so a session saves and reloads it and
   undo removes a stroke.
2. **Operation `mesoscale.paint`** `{ list, target, positions, normals, spin? }` → one commit adding one stroke to the
   list's `ParticleListPainted` (created on first use). One history step per stroke. Orientation: the target's local +z
   along the normal, a random spin about it (the convention mesobuild's membrane targets use: their mid-plane at z = 0);
   for any other target, its principal axis (documented, a param to override).
3. **Gesture: "Paint with …"** in the right-click menu of a particle entity row (or a particle in the canvas): enters a
   paint mode with that entity's target as the brush; Esc or a second click on the item leaves it. A stroke is a
   `drag()` on a canvas overlay: each frame (`frameState`) one `identify` at the pointer and three at ±2 px give the
   position and a normal (the cross product of the neighbours' offsets: works on any representation); points closer than
   the brush spacing to the stroke's last are skipped. Preview: small oriented discs drawn by a transient shape outside
   the state (no state change while drawing); release commits `mesoscale.paint` once.
4. **Properties** show the brush (target, spacing in Å, spin random/fixed, side: along or against the normal) while the
   mode is on.

## v2 (not in this brief): fibres

A stroke becomes a new fibre (the list's `fibers` CSR gains one row of the stroke's points at the fibre's spacing),
drawn by the fibers representation; edit = drag a point (setParticleFrames already moves existing ones); wrap = project
the stroke on a picked object's surface offset by the fibre radius.

## Built

- `ParticleListPainted` (packages/extensions/mesoscale/src/paint.ts): a decorator that appends each stroke's copies to
  the list. Keys go after the largest. Targets, entities, compartments, orientations and radii are extended. Attributes
  read as not present for the copies. Fibres and `targetMapping` are kept. It is registered, so sessions keep it (a
  state-snapshot test). The copies' orientations come from `paintRotations`, with a seed, stored in the params so redo
  and reload are exact.
- `mesoscale.paint` (operations.ts): one stroke per run, one commit, one history step. The first stroke inserts the
  decorator right under the list, with the list's Transform (identity) under it, so a later `stage.move` or
  `stage.scale` carries the copies. Points and normals are mapped into the list's frame. The orientation defaults by
  compartment: a membrane or surface compartment gives +z along the normal, anything else gets a random orientation.
  `axis`, `view`, `side`, `spin` and `seed` are options. Lists whose targets come from a class are refused. The
  operation has a command and an agent tool, `paint_mesoscale_copies`.
- `surfaceAt` (pick-surface.ts): the point and normal under the pointer, from the picks 4 px left, right, up and down.
  It takes the central difference where both sides hit the same surface, else the shorter side, else faces the viewer.
- Paint mode (paint-mode.ts, paint-host.ts):
  - A layer over the canvas in its own scope. The stroke is a `drag()`. Once a frame, the path since the last read is
    sampled every 4 px. A copy goes every spacing (default: a copy's diameter).
  - The preview is discs lying on the surface, drawn on a 2D layer. Nothing is allocated per pointer event.
  - Release commits the stroke. Esc stops the mode, an undo while drawing cancels the stroke, and removing the list or
    disposing the viewer ends the mode. A refused stroke is reported on the status line.
- UI:
  - An entity's right-click: Paint with … / Stop painting (menu.ts).
  - A Paint section in the entity's properties (controls.tsx `PaintBrush`, properties.tsx): Paint/Stop, Spacing, Turn,
    Side, Spin.
- Size: about 1,190 production lines, of which about 920 are code without comments, against the brief's 600–800. The
  extra room went to:
  - the paint mode, about 300 lines: way sampling, pending picks, preview math, lifecycle;
  - the operation's frame mapping and validation;
  - the review's fixes.

## Decided

- Ludo (2026-11-08): the brief's three decisions:
  1. The code lives in the mesoscale extension.
  2. Copies get a random orientation off a membrane; axis and view are options.
  3. No collisions.
- Lane (to confirm with Ludo):
  - On a membrane particle, a membrane protein's brush takes that particle's frame: its +z as the normal, and the point
    moved onto its mid-plane. This deviates from the brief's pure cross product. Why: the pick hits the outer leaflet's
    atoms, so copies would float about 2 nm out, and a normal taken over lipids is noisy. Other brushes keep the picked
    point.
  - The picks for a normal are 4 px apart, not 2. Why: the pick buffer is half resolution.
  - The preview is a 2D layer of projected discs, not a transient 3D shape. Why: a shape in the scene marks the pick
    dirty, and the pick pass would be redrawn over 88k–2M particles at each accepted point.
  - The brush's settings belong to the mode, not to the state (no history step).
  - The Dawn (headless WebGPU) normal test was dropped. Its first picks did not answer reliably here: no hit after a
    camera fit, or sub-Å pixels where the depth precision dominates. The normal is tested on a fake pick of an analytic
    sphere. The real check is the app's (TODO).

## Round 2 (Ludo, 2026-11-09): the camera while painting, a copy at the pointer, soluble and fibre brushes

**Asked (Ludo, after trying v1 on 1352).** "In paint mode we can't control the camera anymore, need a modifier [...]

**Decided (Ludo, 2026-11-09, asked).**

   mode swaps the trackball's bindings for their Alt versions on start and restores them on stop; the layer lets an Alt

## Round 3 (Ludo, 2026-11-09): fibres by rise and twist, an entity's procedural settings

Ludo: "the fiber painting … need to use proper rise and twist for the given element to repeat, and this should be in the
properties. And if I change the properties it should apply to all of the entity, like any procedural system. That way I
can paint DNA (proper twist and rise perfectly known), and RNA (random twist, known rise)."

### Decided (Ludo, 2026-11-09)

1. Changing an entity's fibre settings rebuilds its **painted** fibres only; the fibres the file has stay as it has
   them.
2. Random twist: each step turns by the twist plus a random amount in ±jitter (°), seeded per stroke (repeatable);
   jitter 180 is fully random.
3. The settings (Rise Å, Twist °, Jitter °) are in the entity's properties: a Fibre section of their own, shown for an
   entity of a list (the same controls as the Paint section's brush).

### Design

- A fibre stroke keeps its **path** (the points read along the pointer, `positions`) and a `seed`; its particles are
  made by `ParticleListPainted` from the path and the entity's settings: points every `rise` along the path (by arc
  length, from its first point), each turned about the entity's fibre axis (`fiberConventionOf`'s, else the target's
  principal axis) by the sum of the steps' turns (twist + jitter·u, u in [-1, 1] of the seed and the step).
- `ParticleListPainted.params.fibers`: an entity's (a target's, on a list without entities) `{ rise, twist, jitter }`;
  absent: the entity's own fibres' (`fiberConventionOf`: step, twist, jitter 0), else rise its diameter, twist 0.
- `mesoscale.fiber` (`particles`, `entity`, `rise?`, `twist?`, `jitter?`): sets an entity's settings, one step; every
  painted fibre of it is made again. `fiberState(particles, entity)`: the settings in effect, for views.
- Paint mode reads the rise at each stroke's start (a stroke after a change uses it); `auto` makes a fibre for an entity
  with fibres or with settings.
- Saved sessions of round 2 (strokes with points and rotations): their points are the path, made again at the rise.

### Tests

- `paint.test.ts`: points every rise along a bent path; twist with jitter 0 exactly k·twist about the axis; jitter
  within ±jitter a step and the same for the same seed; settings changed: every painted fibre of the entity made again,
  another entity's not.
- `paint-operation.test.ts`: `mesoscale.fiber` one step (undo puts the old settings back); `fiberState` defaults from

  model/src/formats/structure/mmcif.ts:231) but are not bundled. `data/examples/1bna_confal_pyramids.cif` is a B-DNA
  dodecamer that has all four base pairs.
- No code adds target ids at runtime. `ParticleListWithTargets` (plugin/core/.../particles/ops.ts:370) only remaps
  existing ids, and paint keeps `targetMapping`/`targetInfo` as they are. A structure target is
  `{ kind: 'structure', structure }`, built by `particleTargetFromParsed`
  (plugin/core/src/state/helpers/particle-targets.ts:135).
- Seq* (packages/sequences): `Translator.register` (sequences/core/src/translate/translator.ts:103) gives a host object
  an external coordinate system mapped onto Seq* units. That's the hook for particles. The 3D side is structure-only
  today (`StructureView3D`, `StructureLink` in prism/viewer/src/sequence/molstar/), and nothing maps `Particle.Loci`.

### Design (proposed)

- **A. The library and the switch (this lane):**
  - Unit structures are generated once into a small bundled mmCIF:
    - DNA base pairs: sliced from 1BNA and put in a common frame (helix axis z, the pair's dyad x).
    - RNA nucleotides and amino acids: from CCD ideal coordinates, put in the same frame (backbone along z).
  - `ParticleListPainted` adds a library unit as a new target, with its id after the list's largest. It extends
    `targetInfo` and `targetMapping` together, and only for the units a stroke uses.
  - `FiberSettings.bank`: `'dna' | 'rna' | 'protein'`.
  - `PaintStroke.sequence`: `{ residues, start?, end? }`, or `random`. A fibre's point i gets the unit of letter
    `start + i` (`random`: seeded by the stroke's seed). The sequence restarts on each fibre and repeats when the fibre
    is longer.
  - `brushIdOf` and `fiberOfBrush` keep the stroke's own target as the brush, so a bank unit at point 0 does not break
    the convention lookup.
  - Each particle gets a `seq` attribute holding its sequence index, and its label shows the letter and its number.
- _*B. The Seq* link (a lane of its own, after A):_*
  - A `ParticleLink` on `Translator`: one external system per painted fibre, mapping point i to the sequence's position
    `start + i`.
  - A particle 3D view adapter, so hover and selection go both ways between Seq* and `Particle.Loci`.
  - The fibre's sequence is a Seq* molecule built from its residues (`createSeqViewSpec`'s `sequence`).

### Round 5: the library's source (Ludo, 2026-11-09)

Ludo: units from cellpackgpu (`~/Dev/cellpackgpu/Data/proteins/`) and cellPACK_data (`mesoscope/cellPACK_data`,
`cellPACK_database_1.1.0/other/`), "aligned to the proper forward axis".

- **What exists:**
  - DNA: `dna_AT/TA/GC/CG.pdb` (3DNA v1.6). Each is a base pair centred on the helix axis z, with strand A running 5'→3'
    toward **−z** (`DNA_oneTurn.pdb`: 3.38 Å, −36° about +z a step).
  - RNA: `RNA_U/A/C/G_Base.pdb`, residues 2–5 of one 3DNA strand. Their P atoms are 8.97 Å off the axis z, and each step
    is +36° and +3.38 Å along **+z**.
  - Protein: only `alanine.pdb` (cellpackgpu expects 20 files, PdbLoader.cs:2996), so the protein bank is deferred. I
    won't fetch CCD files without asking Ludo.
- **Decided:**
  - Every unit is stored forward **+z**: the DNA pairs are turned 180° about y (strand A forward; a proper turn, so the
    hand is kept), and each RNA unit is turned back and lowered to step 0.
  - The banks' defaults: rise 3.38 Å, twist +36°, axis z, offset 0. An RNA unit sits about 9 Å off the axis, so the path
    is the helix axis.
  - A unit is a `sites` target (positions, van der Waals radii, elements, chains), generated into a TS data file with
    its source in a header.
  - The order: A (bank, sequence, random) in this lane first; B (the Seq\* link) as its own lane (Ludo did not answer;
    the default).
- **Test that decides the frame:** the normalized units placed by `makeFiber` (a path along z, rise 3.38, twist 36) land
  on the source atoms: RNA U, A, C, G at steps 0–3 on `RNA_*_Base.pdb`, and the DNA pairs on `DNA_oneTurn.pdb`.

### Round 5: built (2026-11-09), part A

- `fiber-units.ts`: the units (DNA pairs from cellpackgpu, RNA nucleotides from cellPACK_data), forward +z, as `sites`
  made once. `fiber-units.test.ts` places them with `makeFiber` (3.38 Å, 36°) back on their sources' P atoms (RNA within
  0.005 Å; DNA within 0.05 Å, because the source's rise alternates between 3.375 and 3.38 Å).
- `paintParticles`:
  - A unit target is one (unit, entity) pair, a `sites` target whose `targetInfo` names the entity.
  - Its id is `base + entity·8 + unit` (base: after the list's largest id), so it stays the same every rebuild.
  - The maps are cached by the input's maps and the set of units used, and passed through unchanged without a bank.
  - Banks apply only on lists with entities.
  - Each point's radius is its unit's reach.
- `mesoscale.fiber` `bank` (choosing one sets its helix unless given); `mesoscale.paint` `sequence`, `start`, `end`; in
  the Fibre section, a Bank pull-down; in Paint, Sequence and Range fields.
- Tests first: 5 in `paint.test.ts`, 1 in `paint-operation.test.ts`, 2 in `paint-host.test.ts`. 341 tests pass.
- In the app (HIV, Genome, WebGL and WebGPU): 42 points of `ATGCATGCGGGCCCAATT`, in order (AT TA GC CG …), each labelled
  with its letter (`· G3`). They form a continuous double helix with its grooves, and RNA turns the same fibre into a
  single strand.
- Not yet: a protein bank (only `alanine.pdb` exists in cellPACK_data); the Seq\* link (part B, its own lane); changing
  a painted fibre's sequence after it is painted (today it is set per stroke, from Paint's fields at the time).
