## Decided

- **No index** (the plan's decision): the browser walks the API and the listings, both `ACAO: *`, under `ebi.ac.uk` (a
  known service: the assistant is not asked).
- **Selects, as the portal's card**, for condition, grid and run, not a folding tree: one pick shows one run's files
  with sizes; the rest of the grid's files that no run claims are listed greyed.
- **Tomogram as slices only**, no direct volume: a full-resolution direct volume is an 875 MB 3D texture.
- **MRCs parsed without the format's visuals** (`Ccp4Provider.parse`): `load.url ccp4` made an isosurface of the
  tomogram only to hide it (seconds and memory at 218 M voxels).
- **Files above 2 GB greyed** (`EmpiarWholeMaxBytes`), not "load anyway": 12534's tilt series are 3.3 GB each, which
  read whole would end the tab after an hour's download. So B.1's tilt series path is built and tested on a small stack,
  but cannot run on 12534 until step C.
- **Sizes as the listing writes them** (MiB, shown as MB: `834 MB`), the total in the same units so it is their sum
  (`1263 MB`); the guard compares bytes.
- **Default files: what loads of the run but its tilt series**, so both STARs. The co-translation picks are a subset of
  the 80S picks (scaled by their own 3.33 Å, 23 of 23 on YTC043_2_lam1_ts_004 fall on an 80S point, 0 Å): drawn twice at
  the same places. Ludo unticks one in the card; not decided for him.
- **Ask first in the card**: the operation's `confirm` shows "…: 1263 MB to read whole (…). Load anyway?", a second Load
  passes `large` (the portal's card passes `large` at once, its size beside Load).
- The run's grid is found from the stem (the grid whose name starts it): 12534's layout; another entry falls back to the
  first grid in the card, or needs `condition`/`grid` in `empiar.load`.

## Learned

- **12534's layout matches the plan's but in counts:** the vehicle condition has 2 grids (YTC009_3, YTC025_2), not 5,
  and its mdocs are in `mdocs/` (CHX: `PACE_mdocs/`); YTC041_1 holds 24 runs. The API says 38 CHX tomograms; the folders
  hold 91, and the 80S STAR names 90 of them (not YTC043_1_lam10_ts_002).
- **Apache sizes are MiB, rounded**: `834M` is 874.7e6 bytes (682×960×334×4); the labels `417M` are int16 (mode 1,
  values 0–2); the tilt series `3.3G`.
- **The co-translation STAR is RELION 3.1** (an optics block, `rlnPixelSize` 3.33, `rlnOriginXAngst`): the reader's
  per-row pixel size puts its picks on the 80S picks (4.99 Å), the same frame.
- **Undoing a load that made a volume and a particle list in one step** raises an unhandled TypeError in prism-viewer's
  scene sync (`sources/scene.ts` `particleListData` → `getDecoratorRoot` on a removed list: the particle hierarchy is
  stale). The portal's `cryoetPortal.load` (`as: slices`, `annotations: 5`) then `history.undo` reproduces it: not this
  lane's; a library lane (a guard on a ref no longer in the tree). The lane's undo test loads volumes only.
- EMDB averages come from the PDBe volume server (`/pdbe/densities/emd/emd-48752/cell?detail=3`).
- Reading the entry from cold (the API and 8+6 listings): 12.8–26 s from EBI; 3 s warm. The quick check (the 80S STAR
  and EMD-48752, no tomogram): 195 s from EBI.

## Fix round (2026-11-05, after the night review `2026-11-05-night/review-cryoet.md`)

**Built.** B2 the download outside the history step (36b7a7ac1); B4/B5/B6/B7 the cards' `LoadAction` and `readingFlag`
in `card-common.tsx` (a3be9c547); B3 one unit, MB of 10^6 bytes (9f27fb68b); EMPIAR warns of a named file that cannot
load (b3d0fa0d4); D1, D2, D3, D5 into `load-scene.ts` (a73662e83, c10857cc1, 038445846, 671fa8bda); then the gate's
findings (table in the PR).

**Decided.** (Ludo's rule, no repetition) the shared module is `load-scene.ts`, not `load-steps.ts`: the cards import
`load-steps.ts` and have no viewer import. Decimal MB everywhere (the portal's, the guard's, the speeds'), kB and GB at
the ends. The card's Load is kept by the area (`LoadAction.of`), so closing the card does not lose a load. A second run
of a STAR gets its own list through `copy`, not a second list under the first's Download. Decided by the agents, for
Ludo to read in the PR.

**Learned.** The gate found what the tests had not: a second run of a condition loaded no particles (the STAR's address
counts as loaded), a shared download was cancelled for both loads and dropped before the second load's step, and the
card lost its load on close. The history's restore hides a missing `quietLoad` cleanup: checked by a probe outside any
step. `Data.ResponseBody` has no release for binary, so a parsed MRC's bytes stay (P2): a library change.
