## Decided

- Design by one Opus subagent (signals, helper signature, golden policy); I followed it. Golden every 8th frame (not 4) to keep
  the inline size about 250 lines.
- The helper lives in `_spec` (jest runs `*.spec.ts` only; tsconfig excludes `_spec`). The three-line drums peak step of
  `updateStems` is copied in the helper, not exported (it is private state of the manager): if it changes, the golden
  must be regenerated.
- The reactor is given the analyser's buffer length (N samples), not `getAudioReactorInputSampleCount`.

## Learned

- `analyze` reads the OLDEST `inputSampleCount` samples of the analyser buffer: `ambient-pulse` (fft 2048 at 24 kHz) reads audio
  about 78 ms old at 48 kHz (0 ms at 44.1 kHz). A lag for `heard-frame`.
- The app overrides a preset's `beatThreshold` with its knob (1.1), so ambient-pulse's 1.25 never applies; the helper uses 1.1.
- `bandDrumsPeak` is not reset by stop or clear.
- The repo's eslint parser rejects `??`, optional catch binding, `import.meta` and top-level await in `.mjs`.
- Headless the output delay is 33 ms; the drive peaks about when the kick is heard (heard lag about 0 +- 1 frame).
