## Decided

- Design by one Opus subagent (signals, helper signature, golden policy); I followed it. Golden every 8th frame (not 4) to keep
  the inline size about 250 lines.
- The helper lives in `_spec` (jest runs `*.spec.ts` only; tsconfig excludes `_spec`). The helper drives the real
  manager `tick` (review item 1), so a change to `tick`, `updateStems` or the router shows in the golden by itself.
- The fake analyser's buffer is the manager's own (`analyser.fftSize` set by `setParams`), N samples ending at the media cursor.

## Learned

- `analyze` reads the OLDEST `inputSampleCount` samples of the analyser buffer: `ambient-pulse` (fft 2048 at 22050 Hz) reads audio
  about 78 ms old at 48 kHz (0 ms at 44.1 kHz). A lag for `heard-frame`.
- The app overrides a preset's `beatThreshold` with its knob (1.1), so ambient-pulse's 1.25 never applies; the helper uses 1.1.
- `bandDrumsPeak` is not reset by stop or clear.
- The repo's eslint parser rejects `??`, optional catch binding, `import.meta` and top-level await in `.mjs`.
- Headless the output delay is 33 ms; the drive peaks about when the kick is heard (heard lag about 0 +- 1 frame).

## For lane sound-stops (from review item 2)

The spec row is: every drive exactly 0 within the longest release + 1 frame = 400 + 16 ms after a pause; the test samples at 3.416 s for a
pause at 3 s. A cut-off of 1e-3 on the route values alone cannot meet it: the routes' releases are exponential (400 ms for illumination,
so 0.35 of the held value is still there at 416 ms), and the band envelope's release feeds the route's release. The sources (the stems
and bands the routes read) must go to 0 on pause, and the route state must not release from a held value, or the value must be forced to
0. The two "today" tests in the spec (the 1.5 s tail, the wiggle floor) pin today's behaviour; sound-stops deletes them.
